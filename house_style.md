## Style preferences
- Transliteration in plain letters: no macrons, dots or ʿ (Husain not Ḥusayn, Ali not ʿAlī).
- Poetry: short, dignified hemistichs; keep the images of the Arabic (ark, musk, spring, sun, moon) rather than
  paraphrasing them away; refrains identical in every stanza.
- Dua: "Allah, ..." or "O Allah, ..." as the examples do; second person "You/Your" for Allah.
- Instruction lines (Lisan al-Dawat): concise imperative English ending with a colon when a dua follows.
