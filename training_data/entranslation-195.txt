How many hidden graces Allah has,=That are concealed even from intelligent minds,
How many good times come after difficult times,=As Allah dispels the grief of the troubled heart,
How many difficulties trouble You in the morning,=And yet happiness follows in the evening,
If the difficulties of the times surround You one day,=Keep trusting in Allah, the One and Exalted,
Seek intercession (wasila) of the Nabi,=Because every tragedy becomes light if the Nabi’s intercession (wasila) is sought,
Do not worry when faced with hardships,=As Allah Ta’ala has many hidden graces,
