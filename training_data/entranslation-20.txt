Glory to your lord, the Mighty Lord, free from the things they ascribe to Him,
and salaams to the prophets. All praise to Allah, Lord of the Worlds.
Blessed is Allah, best of creators. There is no strength and no might save from Allah, Elevated, Great.
Lord, accept our prayers and supplications, O Lord, O Mawla, you are All hearing, All knowing.
Do not throw them back at our faces, O God of the worlds, O Best of helpers. Be merciful, O merciful one.
Lord, pray salawaat on Mohammad and his family.
