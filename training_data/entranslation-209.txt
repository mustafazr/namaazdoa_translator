Then pray this
Allah, my lord, my master: If I obey you with recognition and knowledge, you have given this gift to me. If I disobey you in my ignorance, you have a claim against me. 
I ask you to bestow on me your gift and seek from me your claim—but no, have mercy on me! 
Do not deprive me from the joy of seeing your beloved one, your chosen one, Muhammad, and his Wasi Ali Amirul Mumineen—may you shower your salawaat upon them in your noble abode.
Do not deprive me from the joy of seeing  your beloved one, your chosen one, Imam ul zaman, and his Dai - may you shower your salawaat upon them in your noble abode.
Do not deprive me from the joy of seeing  your beloved two, your chosen two, Syedna Taher Saifuddin, and his waris Syedna Muhammad Burhanuddin - may you shower your salawaat upon them in your noble abode.
Allah, my good deeds please you and my bad deeds do not harm you. Help me do what pleases you and forgive me for doing what does not harm you
O generous one—shower on me your generosity, O most generous lord.
Allah, my lord, my master: The world is made delightful only with your remembrance. 
The hereafter is made delightful only with your forgiveness. 
Days are made delightful only when spent in your obedience.
Daylight hours are made delightful only when spent in your service. Nights are made delightful only when spent in beseeching you. 
Hearts are made delightful only when blessed by love for you. Paradise is made delightful only with pardon from you
This world and the next are made delightful only through you.  Do all this with your mercy, O most merciful one.
