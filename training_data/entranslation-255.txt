After completing Washeq Namaaz, lay your right cheek on the masalla and recite 40 times:
This prayer is my gift to you, Maulana Mohammed, and to you, Maulatuna Fatema. Accept it from me and give me your manifold blessings in return.
Thereafter, lay your left cheek on the masalla and recite the same 40 times:
This prayer is my gift to you, Maulana Mohammed, and to you, Maulatuna Fatema. Accept it from me and give me your manifold blessings in return.
