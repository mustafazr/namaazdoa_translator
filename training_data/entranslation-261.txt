After finishing the namaaz of ‘ibtighaa’il fadli wath thawaab’ (‘seeking virtue and reward’) do the following tasbeeh:
Allah is all transcendent, all praise is for Allah, I seek the forgiveness of Allah, there is no God but Allah, and God is the Greatest. All is only that which Allah wills, there is no power and no strength except through Allah, Allah is my suffice, I cling to Allah, I put my trust in Allah.

Then recite this dua:
My face prostrates to You, my neck lowers itself before You in meekness, then accept my repentance, have mercy on my weakness, and my poverty and my deprivation and my distress and misery.
For You I kept my fast, and from Your provision I ate, and upon You I put my trust.
Salaam upon you O month of Ramadaan, Salaam upon you O month of Ramadaan, Salaam upon you O month of Ramadaan.
You were a fortress for he who read the Quran through your duration, and he who worshipped the All Merciful through your duration.
How many a person who fasts but his fast does not count ever! How many a person who stands before you in prayer but his prayer does not count ever!
Allah is our suffice, and He is our best keeper for all our matters, and He is our helper.
And there is no power and no strength except through Allah, The Elevated, The Great.
O Allah, pray salawaat on Mohammed and the progeny of Mohammed and bless them and pray salaam upon them.
