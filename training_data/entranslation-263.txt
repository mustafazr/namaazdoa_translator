I have been born to offer my life in the service of the descendant of Mustafa, Chief of the Prophets=how, then, would I have any other occupation?!
O descendant of Ali, father of the Prophet’s grandsons, O descendant of Fatema,=I am your humble servant, come to you as a tiny ant (“naml” is a reference to Surat al-Naml 27:18, and the Quran’s story of an ant coming into Prophet Sulayman’s presence)
You are the heir of your pure father who wielded the Dhu l-Faqar sword.=You are the sword of right, truth, and justice.
Tayyib Imam! Descendant of Imam Abul-Qasim Tayyib!=You are pure, tayyib, in branch and root.
It is your heavenly abode, O Imam of the Age,=toward which I urge my camels, in order to have my petitions answered.
O Imam who is himself Eid ul Fitr, Eid ul Adha, Eid-e Ghadir-e Khumm.=O you whose person is the true Eid!
You are unique in your high station.=You are lofty beyond any peer who could resemble you
The true Qur’an speaks about you and through you.=You are its full meaning, the one signified
O Ka’bah, the true Baytullah al Haraam!=O place of descent of Allah’s flowing knowledge from the realm of the intellect.
You are truly Allah’s rope, Habl Allah.=I swear by Allah that I have held fast to his rope!
The light from your forehead gives the sun its light,=as Syedna Mu’ayyad declared authoritatively in his poem.
Your knowledge heals the heart, the soul, and the mind.=It cures the heart’s blindness, it cures ignorance.
My heart is tranquil because it always remembers you.=It is captivated by love for you, O you who gathers Mumineen in one united group!
My best occupation is praising you.=I recite your praise as I recite the Qur’an, and it increases my stature.
Love for you bequeaths your servant a triumphant entry to Paradise.=Enmity toward you burns your enemies in their Fire.
Through your grace, Allah’s Wali,=we rise to the higher realm from this lower world.
You, my master, are the giver of victory and aid.=Your flowing inspiration, ta’eed, sharpens my sword.
I fight a group who have broken their pledge of misaaq.=Grant me, by your grace, aid and victory in this battle!
Raise high the minaret of your radiant Dawat.=Destroy the enemies of your faith—strike them down.
O refuge of the refuge seeker, O sanctuary of all the people,=O shelter in times of distress and anguish!
Help your stricken servant, O my Maula, quickly!=Else the Age will strike me down and humiliate me.
I care not what storms the Age throws at me,=when you, my lord, are the one who will dispel harm, dispel drought.
O Saba wind, take my greetings, with my prostrations,=to the abode of the descendant of Murtaza, “king of the bees”. (reference to Amirul Mumineen’s words; bees symbolize Mumineen).
Present to him my felicitations for Eid ul Fitr!=Ask him, by his grace, to raise my station!
I am in need only of his kind glances.=I do only what he directs me to do.
I am Taher, Qutbul Huda’s appointed successor, Mansoos.=His radiant face, like the full moon, dispelled the darkness.
I am the servant of Mustafa’s descendants,=and heir of all the Dais—people of lofty statue and virtue—who came before me.
May Allah shower salawaat on Taha Nabi and his descendants,=and on their representatives, Na’ib, of whom I am one.
