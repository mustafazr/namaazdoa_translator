The best people in the world are Muhammad, his Wasi Ali, the pious Fatima Zahra, Hasan and Husayn.
Through them, blindness has been dispelled, and the world, after perplexity and confusion, has received guidance.
Our prayers are not complete except through allegiance to them; it is their praise that is called out at every call to prayer.
They are the guardians of Allah’s knowledge, the recipients of his revelation, the ones who explain the doctrines of faith.
They are the banners of Allah’s religion, the foundations of guidance, the ships of salvation, the pillars of faith.
They are Allah’s rope—whoever clings to it is saved, while the one who holds back falls to perdition.
I came to Karbala; my grief was rekindled, and sorrow burned in my heart.
I remembered my Maula’s killing on Aashura; O my grief upon his thirst!
I weep for Husayn and his companions with blood and unceasing tears.
I weep for the one killed in Karbala, lying on the ground exposed without a shroud.
I weep for Muhammad’s grandson, an exile in Taff, alone, far away from his homeland.
I weep for Muhammad’s daughters, made captive through oppression and malice.
What defense will the killer of Muhammad’s family offer Allah at the Resurrection?
Allah is not unaware of the wrong perpetrated on his servants; a momentous day awaits them.
May Allah shower Salawaat-blessings on the Prophet and his descendants as long as the turtle dove warbles on the bough.
