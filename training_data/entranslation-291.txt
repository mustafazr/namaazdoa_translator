O Naseem wind, go to the courtyard of the generous Hatim, ocean of bounty, who fulfils hopes, who grants wishes.
Convey my greetings to him, laying your cheek humbly in the dust of his shrine.
Hark! Hatim is the sun among all the great Dais appointed by Maulatuna Arwa in the Imam’s Concealment.
How many shining virtues Hatim possesses! How many dazzling miracles are narrated about him!
There is many a blind person to whom Hatim has bestowed sight. There is many a leper whom he has cured. There is many a dead person whom he has brought to life. There is many a thirsty person whose thirst he has quenched.
Hatim gives refuge to the refuge seeker. He dispels grief and calamity.
O you who are struck by grief, take refuge in Hatim’s shelter! He will remove all your sorrows.
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Help me! Help me! O you whom the Imam has chosen! O lofty Hatim, O generous Hatim!
Allah, accept Hatim’s intercession on my behalf. By his sanctity, O Glorious One, answer my prayer.
Grant longest life to our Dai Syedna Fakhruddin. He is the strongest proof of Syedna Qutbuddin’s righteousness.
 
 
May the Lord of the Throne preserve Syedna Fakhruddin in the shade of His blessings. May his enemies burn in hell.
May Allah shower salawaat blessings on Taha Nabi and his descendants. They are the greatest blessing. They are the final destination.
