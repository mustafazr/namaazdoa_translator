My soul goes on Hajj to Husain in Karbala.=My heart melts with his remembrance.	
I have come on Aashura day with this plea:=O King of Martyrs, be my refuge!
O son of Fatima and Haydar! O grandson of the one=whom the Lord of the World sent as Prophet!	
O light of truth, sun of the morning, moon of the night, glow of guidance!=O beloved of Mumineen!	
I grieve for you, O Allah’s Wali.=You were slain thirsty in Karbala.
I weep for you, who were killed unjustly in Taff.=My tears flow like a waterfall.	
My eyes are a gushing spring. My cheeks are a riverbed.=My weeping body shakes and trembles.	
When you were slain, the sun was eclipsed,=and the moon wept in the sky.	
I grieve for you, soul of Muhammad’s religion!=My soul is clothed in sorrow! 	
I mourn Zaynul Abedeen,=shackled with irons on the burning sands of Taff.
I mourn, I grieve, for Zaynab’s weeping, as she cries:=O where is Husain? Who will dispel my hardship?	
I mourn for Husain’s beloved Abbas and Ali,=and for the suckling baby who shuddered upon his hands.	
The Prophet has promised Paradise=for those who weep tears in remembrance of your tragedy.
No doubt, Allah will fulfil the wishes of those=who take your name, O Husain.
Allah, grant Mumineen their wishes, by Husain’s wasila,=and, in your mercy, erase their sins.	
I petition Husain: O refuge of the helpless,=O shelter, O fortress in times of difficulty!
Fulfil our wishes, by your kind benevolence!=Dispel the hardships of all who are in turmoil!
By your kindness, grant permission for me, your servant,=to enter the Paradise that is Karbala.
O companion, this religion lives on because of Husain.=Remember Husain whenever you hear a muezzin chant the name of God.	
Have you not read the Wise Remembrance?=The Lord of the World has revealed its verses in remembrance of Husain.	
Allah has continued the unbroken chain=of the Imamate in Husain’s line.
Their Dais are among us, like Syedna Taher Saifuddin,=who was crowned with the Imam’s inspiration.	
Of all radiant Awliya,=he was the best reciter of Aqa Husain’s martyrdom.	
His successor, Syedna Muhammad Burhanuddin,=also eloquently recited the martyrdom of Husain among the people.	
His Mazoon is Qutbuddin Maula—=no one in the whole world possesses the like of his trust in God.
May the Lord of the World keep both Maulas in good health for all time.=I pray to Allah with sincerity and hope.
May Allah shower Salawaat on Taha Nabi and his descendants.=By mandating love for them, Allah has perfected his religion.	
O Naseem breeze, convey my humble greetings=in Karbala to the martyrs of Taff.
