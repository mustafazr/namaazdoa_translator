The radiant, noble Imams in Nabi Mustafa’s progeny are our Mawaali (pl. of Mawla)=Because of their endeavors, our exaltedness shines forth in all the worlds
















Among the Imams is Allah’s elect, Tayyib of our Age=Who has brought might and beauty to His religion






In your concealment (satr), O Imams, you have appointed true Dais=They are your proofs manifest (burhaan-s) in all the lands




















I am Taher Saifuddin, your servant=I have come to you, pure son of Mustafa, begging your favor
Help us, Waliyullah, we are your servants=One kind glance from you is all we need

Allah, grant me a long life to foster the Dawat=Of the Imams in the progeny of Murtaza and Yaseen (Rasulullah’s name)

Allah, increase the True Dawat’s magnificence=Grant me your aid (ta’eed) and increase my support
O Allah, grant a long life to Burhanuddin, proof of your religion [O Allah, sanctify the soul of Burhanuddin, proof of your religion]=Who has attained abundant luminous virtues
He has attained the form willed by Allah and Imamuz zaman,=Son of Mustafa—and the form that I willed
The Lord of the Throne has ornamented him with noble virtues=And lofty qualities, alongside humility and knowledge
Mohammed is praised by the whole world=There is no need to describe his lofty virtues—mentioning his name suffices
We give thanks to our Mawla, Lord of our Age=He is the one who grants us true life
O Lord, let me and my noble sons=Revel forever in the flowerbeds of your favors
We ask the lord of Kawthar, Murtaza=A drink from its celestial pool
Allah, include in all my prayers=All mumineen, those who obey me—they are my children
Make their trade profitable, make their industry thrive=Grant them security from all things that harm
May the Lord of the World shower salawaat on Taha and his pure progeny=As long as rain showers bring gardens to life
