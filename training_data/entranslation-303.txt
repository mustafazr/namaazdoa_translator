Salaam to you Mawla Burhanuddin!=Dai of the Merciful, noble, luminous.
Salaam to you, Dai of Rasulullah!=You explained his Shariat to the people.
Salaam to you, light of Haidar!=Loving him was your faith.
Salaam to you, Imam Tayyeb’s Dai!=You proclaimed his religion to the world.
Salaam to you, son of Taher!=You are his qurrat ul ayn.
Salaam to you, light of Allah!=Goldmine of piety and virtue.
Salaam to you, treasure trove of knowledge!=From the vault of secrets belonging to Rasulullah’s progeny.
Salaam to you, loving, and revered father!=Our refuge.
A great Dai!=A pure, auspicious Bab, Mu’ayyad, for Rasulullah’s son.
A great guide!=You directed the world to the straight path.
A great Mawla, Mohammad!=Granting favors to the world, gifts to mankind
A great savior!=Saving the souls of believers from the fire.
A great giver of life!=For the hearts of all who come seeking to be healed by his guidance
A great elevator of intellects!=Transporting them to safety in the highest peaks of God’s abode.
A great masjid-builder, worshiper!=Built many masjids, tirelessly worshipped the Merciful.
He brought to life the Jame masjids of the Fatimid Imams=Proclaiming their magnificence to all.
Anwar, Azhar and Aqmar=Longing and yearning for him.
O morning Saba breeze=Carry my humble salaam to the grave of Mawla Burhanuddin
O Allah, sanctify his soul=Give him sanctuary in the shade of Taha’s progeny
Sanctify his grave=At the side of his pure father, in that flowerbed of Paradise, Rauzat Tahera
Burhanuddin Mawla appointed me by the ilhaam of his Mawla, Imam uz zamaan=Proof (burhan) for faith (deen)	
He performed Nass on me, the servant of Aal e Muhammad=Appointing me in his own high station (rutba)
I offer abundant shukur and thanks to him=I will always offer shukur and thanks to him
My soul is fida for my Mawla Tayyeb=My utmost wish is to obtain his flowing Faiz
In all life’s difficulties, my Imam uz zamaan is my refuge=His protection fortifies the stronghold of right guidance
Allah, strengthen my footing in the earth of the Imam’s Dawat Just as you strengthened Yusuf’s footing in the earth  (Reference to Quran Surat Yusuf v. 56)
O Mighty One, grant us victory!=We place our trust in you, we offer you praise and thanks
Bring to speedy justice all enemies of your religion=Like nahr of budn in Mina
Ordain the zuhoor of the Prophet’s son=When, O when, shall we see the sun rise! For its time has come
May God bestow salawaat on the pure Aale Mohammad=Fruits of true knowledge are plucked from their garden.
