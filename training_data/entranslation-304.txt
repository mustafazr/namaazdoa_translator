O most glorious Dai, renowned among all,=O patron more generous than all others, be they Arab or Persian
O Qutbuddin Maula, Dai of the Lord of the World,=Refuge for mumineen in times of hardship and suffering
O son of Maulana Saifuddin, Mansoos of Maulana Burhanuddin,=Your forehead gleams in the darkness like the full-moon
O heir of all the noble Duaat Mutlaqeen,=Your virtues are too exalted to be captured by the pen
My master, you are the vicegerent, Naib,=of the Imams, Your hands heal the sick and the troubled
You are a haven for people in times of tribulation,=O dispeller of grief, O disbander of cares
O peak of majesty, you are the sky of glory,=O banner of piety, O possessor of angelic qualities
You guided us to the path of God’s faith,=You taught us how to worship him inside the sanctuary and out
The Lord of the World granted you the gift of decisive judgment,=Through knowledge and wisdom, you always commemorated Him
Today is the anniversary of your auspicious birth,=The sky of good fortune celebrates with generous rain
O master, accept my felicitations on your pure Milad,=Grace me with your favor and bounty
O Lord, grant our Dai-z-zamaan longest life,=Shade him with your favors, with health and wellbeing
Allah, shower barakaat on him on his birthday,=A day of celebration for all people and all nations
Shower salawaat on Mustafa and his descendants,=As long as Adam’s children walk on this earth
Who can stop me from praising Maulana Qutbuddin,=His love runs in my blood
My life, my wealth, I’ll expend in his service,=With my sword, with my tongue, I’ll slay his enemies
