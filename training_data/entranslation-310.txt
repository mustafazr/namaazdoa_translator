Salaam to you, Mawla Qutbuddin Shaheed=Dai of the Merciful, ocean of bounty
Salaam to you, Bab (‘gate’) of our Imam=His Hujjat (‘proof’), most noble guide
Salaam to you, Mawla, Sage=Dai: Calling us to Allah with His permission
Salaam to you, Dai of Allah=Martyr: Singularly honored with shahadat
A vicious tyrant and his cohort killed you o mazloom=They will burn in the flames of Hell
O morning Saba breeze=Carry my humble salaam to Qutbuddin Mawla
Three Dais in the lofty Qubbah=With Mawla Qutbuddin, best exemplar:
Syedna Qasim-Khan, Syedna Fir-Khan=Syedna Husamuddin, faith’s unsheathed sword
Five more, noble, illustrious Dais=Each with his own sublime Qubbah:
Syedna Jalal, Syedna Daud, Syedna Daud=Syedna Safiyuddin, Syedna Zakiyuddin, each unique in station
Mawlana Qutbuddin is a martyr, Shaheed=Like Husain, martyred in Karbala
I built a Zarih for Husain in Karbala=And a Qubbah for his Dai, Qutb ul huda
I am Taher Saifuddin, Mawla Qutbuddin’s successor=Dai for the pure progeny of the Prophet Ahmad ul Mustafa
May Allah’s salawaat pour down on Nabi Taha and his progeny=And their radiant Dais—as long as the moon shines bright
