Nuruddin Mawla! Whosoever seeks you=Obtains more than he had hoped for
You are our sanctuary, you are our safe haven=In all that hurts us, in calamities and mishaps

Protection for any who seek refuge with him=Asylum for any who beg shelter from him

The most perfect among guides=All perfect in their expositions
Select of the Imam - the son of Nabi Mustafa=Sword of the Imam - the son of Ali Murtaza
