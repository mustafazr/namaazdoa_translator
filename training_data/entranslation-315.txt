Ali, namesake of the Lord of the Great Throne=His eternal light
Crowned by the Lord of Greatness=With the crown of heavenly kingship and divine power
I swear an oath on the Lord of all the Worlds=That you are Mohammad’s wasi
His brother, vizier, khalifa=Honored son-in-law, devotee
You climbed [Mohammad’s] shoulders=Surrounded by noble angles
You ascended the Ka’ba, Baitullah il haraam=Fully deserving of this high honor
You smashed the idols falsely placed there=They came tumbling down
His devotees will dwell in Paradise=Forever enjoying its celestial fruits
Eternal bliss for a man who walks the straight path=Revering his lofty station
His enemies will inhabit Hell=Burning in its fire for aeons
The man who demeans and the man who exaggerates=Both have forged a lie
Do not worship Satan in your hatred of Ali=Satan disobeyed the Merciful
He, yes he, is Allah’s light (noor-e-ilahi)=That shone brightly in Adam’s face
He, yes he, is the Great Report (naba’-e-azeem)=That our Nabi was informed about
Mohammad performed Nass on him=In Ghadir-e-Khumm, A Nass as clear as day
Fatema Zahra was his true match=As he was hers
The Lord would not have unrolled the earth=If not for him, nor raised up the skies
The Surat of “Did it come?” (Hal Ata)—Did it come in another’s praise?=Do you see a noble warrior his equal?
Do you see his peer among the Prophet’s Companions=In goodness, shining virtues, and piety?
Do you see another complaint-receiver=Who answers the call of the distressed?
If you wish for relief from calamity=Call out, “Ya Ali Madad!”
O lofty Mawla, O Amir of mumineen=Give speedy succor to mumineen’s cries for help
O Saqi-ye-Kawthar!=Give me a full cup to drink
Triumphant is the mumin=Who remembers Mawla Ali in the morning and night
O Naseem breeze, carry my humble salaam=To my Mawla in Ghari (Najaf)
I am a devotee of Mustafa and Murtaza=Of Zahra, Hasan and Husain
Of the True Imams in their progeny=The choicest portion of mankind
May Allah’s salawaat encompass the Prophet, and Haidar, and their progeny=As long as Haqq remains exalted (Ali)
