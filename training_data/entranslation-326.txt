The greatest calamity has struck God’s religion=With the passing of a Dai who attained the highest station
Syedna Taher Saifuddin—his loftiest virtue, this:=He was a sun among all the Dais of Taha’s progeny.
He was a sheltering cave for believers, refuge for followers=Truly, in all kinds of catastrophes.
He was the House of Allah—whosoever invoked salawaat on him,=Knowing his high rank, truly performed salaat (prayer) toward Baitullah.
He was a sword (saif) in the hand of the Wasi’s son=Drawn, sharpened, to slay rancorous enemies.
Rainclouds poured down when they saw his face=I swear by this and it is true; I have seen it myself.
What a luminous face, face of Imam uz zamaan=Its light dispelling the blackest darkness.
He built Qasre-Saifee in Mecca, and this Saifee Masjid=All from his personal earnings.
He built the Zarih of Maula Ali and his son Imam Husain=They glitter like heavenly lights.
I grieve him, now hidden in a grave=Like a setting sun that hides in the West.
Let us recite a verse from his qasida=A qasida whose lines are gleaming stars
“Our beginnings have been the best of beginnings due to our Mawali=And by their wasila, we pray for the best end”.
We saw him—in his splendid return—pure light=Ascending to the gathering place of all lights.
He passed away and I stood next to him=Asking him to help me bear this unbearable grief.
I laid my father, my Maula, to rest in his grave=My lord, my benefactor, who gave me all I wished for.
I saw—and I say this with utter conviction—that his grave=Was a flowerbed (rauzat) of paradise, surrounded on all sides by light.
O how many radiant shaanaat he emanated=How many dazzling, astonishing miracles.
I prostrated before him always, and do so now at his grave=Taking solace in beseeching him
O Saba breeze, go to the grave of Maulana Taher=Ocean of generosity, granter of all wishes and hopes
Prostrate before his grave, and convey to my father=The greetings of a son who weeps in his sorrow
But I must endure—and I follow the example=Of the essence of creation, pure sons of the Wasi
I will continue to be grateful to him, abundantly, unceasingly=I will continue to seek his flowing faiz.
He appointed me as his successor by the Imam’s inspiration (ilhaam)=And through me—even though he has gone—he is still present
Imam uz zamaan is my Maula and master=I am his servant, a humble chamberlain
Imam uz zamaan is God’s rope, habl-ullah, so cleave to him=And you will win salvation from the darkness of this world
O Maula Imam uz zamaan, rescue your servant, glance at him=Give him your highest gift of ta’eed.
I am your lowly servant, a supplicant=I urge my steeds toward your favors.
I receive God’s covenant (misaaq) from those=Who believe in God, the best faith.
I am a Dai toward Nabi Muhammad’s religion=Calling to it, just like Syedna Taher Saifuddin
O children of my Dawat, come to me, answer my call=And God will grant all your wishes.
Lo! Come remember Maula Saifuddin with me, your brother=Who is immersed, absorbed, in remembrance.
Whosoever beseeches him, sincere in his love, will be saved=Whosoever denies his standing will be thwarted.
May rainclouds with clear droplets=Pour down with salawaat on Taha and his progeny.
