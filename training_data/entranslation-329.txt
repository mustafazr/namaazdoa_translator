I call to you in munajaat, my lord, you are the one who answers our prayers=With my heart and my soul, I place my hope in you=(‘Munajaat’ means ‘speaking privately’ with Allah).
I have come to you in humility=A care-worn mendicant (faqeer), weighed down by his labors
I have come to you repentant and contrite=I supplicate you, I pray to you
I am the littlest of the little in value=O my lord, O majestic one
I am the humblest of the humble=My errors are many, my shame little
My crime is great, my sin large=I seek your forgiveness, O you who hears our call
Forgive my sins, conceal my faultsErase my transgressions, O creator of the world
Allah, grant me direction, tawfeeq,=Which guides me in all my affairs
In your name, O lord of majesty, grant me refuge=In your name, grant me protection from all calamities
Perfume my life with an honorable character=Fill my heart with knowledge and guidance
Provide abundant sustenance (rizq) to your humble servant=O God, make him wealthy with your riches
Grant my prayers=Complete your blessings upon me. Remove my hardships
O God, grant your protection to me=To my property, my family, my children, and all the people of loyalty
Hardship after hardship, adversity after adversity=To you I complain of the harsh blows of the world
O lord, clothe me in garments of piety, taqwa=Grant my body good health and free it from every disease
Help me to perform acts of charity and kindness=Guard me, O creator of the world, from pretension
I profess loyalty and love for Ali=O creator of the world, give me a full cup from the pool of Kawthar
Grant me coolness of the eyes, qurrat-ul-ain, in my childrem=Nourish them with the most nutritious food
Allah, unite the community of faith=Under the banner of the True Dawat
Make my Dawat children partners in my supplication=I am your servant, na’ib of the aale ‘aba, the Progeny of Rasulullah
I supplicate you in The Night of Power, Laylatul Qadr=O Allah, presenting to you the sanctity of the ahlul-kisa (panjetan) for intercession
The Five Pure Ones=Their virtues shine forth like the sun
Their first is Ahmad, Mustafa=Our own Muhammad, chief of all the prophets
Maula Ali is Rasulullah’s wasi=His heir, chief of all the wasi-s
The pure lady Fatima Zahra is The Night of Power, Laylatul Qadr=Great is her glory, she is the best of all women in the world
Their sons: Hasan, chosen one, mujtaba=And the fifth, chief of martyrs, sayyid-ush-shuhada
My tears rain down when I remember Husain=And his martyrdom on the soil of Karbala
I will remember him morning and night=I will weep for him all through the age
I bring their intercession, vasila, to the lord of the world=And the vasila of their sons, my awliya’
Righteous Imams, lords of truth=Embodiments of light—they are my intercessors
They are my preparation, my refuge, my treasure=They are my sanctuary—my ascension is through them
I take the vasila of Tayyib Imam, master of both cycles, manifestation and concealment=Maula of the world, essence of all essences
And the vasila of their heir, Tayyib of this age,=Best of the world, bestower of blessings
I take the Imams’ vasila, and the vasila of their na’ibs=In this time of Dawat’s winter
Noble Dais, given the Imam’s full authority, mutlaqeen=Trustworthy shepherds guarding the rightly guided flock
I take the vasila of their heir, Maulana Qutbuddin=His arrival in paradise increased its light manifold
He was Maulana Burhanuddin’s appointed successor, mansoos=The best of the world, the most noble and honorable
His father Maulana Taher Saifuddin said of him:=Khuzaima is mustajaab-ud-dua, Allah answers his prayers
He strove to serve the progeny of the noble prophet=Always, and gave his life for them
He established and clarified the chain (silsila) of nass=That is given by one noble Dai to another
His departure has pained my heart=I long to see him again
He remains alive=The hearts of those who love him remember him with sincere affection
May the lord of the world sanctify his soul=And grant him the best of rewards in paradise
He appointed me his successor, by nass, so that I may serve the prophet’s progeny=My splendor and my glory derive solely from them
I will offer him my gratitude as long as I live=For my Maula granted me the highest of gifts
I am his son, Taher=Heir to the legacy of the pious, noble Dais
I am the Dai of the progeny of Ali=With God’s grace, I offer my life for them
I follow their footsteps, praising them=I walk in their path, always
O Allah, this is my prayer to you:=Help me to raise the banners of your faith in the East and the West
Grant me a long life, O lord of the world=To serve my Maula Tayyib Imam
With conviction I walk the path of truth=My house is built on religion’s foundation
This is my prayer, O Allah: Send us=Glad tidings of your mighty aid and a clear victory, nasre-azeez and fathe-mubeen
Seize your religion’s enemies, losers and wretches=Punish them with a painful punishment
May Allah shower salawaat on Taha Nabi’s progeny=Every morning and every evening
