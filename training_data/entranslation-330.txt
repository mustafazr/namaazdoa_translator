I call in munajaat to the lord of the throne, exalted in majesty=I call in supplication to the lord of mercy, whose gifts sustain the world=(‘Munajaat’ means ‘speaking privately’ with Allah).
I call to you in munajaat, Allah, in all humbleness=I am a bondsman who relies only on you
I call to you in munajaat, in worship and tearful entreaty=I am a man hit by calamity who asks for your help
Have mercy on one who has come to you, Allah, with anticipation=To you I direct my camel-caravan, in your court all hopes will be fulfilled
I am a fearful and humble servant standing at your door=I have no virtues, all my deeds are wicked
I am a sinner standing at your door=My desires overcome my fear to transgress, this is my piteous state
My sins are many, pardon them Allah=I will die of shame if their frightful outcomes confront me
In the name of pure Fatema, and in her night, Laylatul Qadr=Forgive your lowly servant=I beseech you, lord of the world
Provide to your servant lawful sustenance (rizq e halaal)=In abundance without limit=Through your kind generosity
Bestow on me favor after favor=For your bounty is unbounded
Adorn me with the splendor of true knowledge=And with the most beautiful character
Allah, grant me qurratul ain in my children=Cover my family always with your blessings
My beginning was a beginning of pure good=Grant me the same for my end
Grant barakat to all who pray namaaz and give zakaat=Make their trade profit richly, let their wealth increase amply
With your mighty aid grant me victory=I am your servant, I fight in defense of your religion
Bring down your enemy and destroy him=He has led many into error
Grant tawfeeq to those who were once mumin=Guide them to join their rope to your firm rope (hablullah il mateen)
By God, my love for Taha Nabi’s Progeny, Imams and their Dais=Perfects my faith
Allah, your Nabi Taha, his Wasi, and their Progeny=Are my vasila, my intercessors, when I pray to you
Their Naibs, the Dais are also my vasila=Each possesses angelic station and noble virtues
They are Allah’s Awliya, saints, our intercessors=Among them is Maulana Qutbuddin who has no peer
I remember him every moment and offer him thanks=Has he left us? No! His countenance, his majesty, continues to blaze!
He has departed for Jannat ul Firdaus, Yet from his station in Paradise=His compassionate grace encompasses us in its cool shade
He performed Nass on me, servant of Mohammed’s Progeny=This is a rope that will never fray asunder
Imam uz zamaan’s light shines forth=I fast, and I break my fast, by the vision of this true crescent
I am his servant, I offer my life for him=He is always close, linked to me through his ta’eed
With his direction and fayz, I guide the believers=My word in all things is his word
May Allah shower salawaat on Taha Nabi and his Progeny=And on their Na’ibs, the Dais, men of truth, “Rijalu Sidq”
