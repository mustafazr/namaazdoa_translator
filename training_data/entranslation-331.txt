I call to you in prayer and munajaat, O Allah, lord of the worlds=The munajaat of Musa, praying to you in difficult times=(Munajaat means speaking privately with Allah, praying to Allah)
The munajaat of our Imam Ali Zayn ul Abedeen=Praying in these long nights for the preservation of Deen=(Imam Zaynul Abedeen used to pray a thousand rak’ats at night. He knew that the rule of the Banu Umayya would last for a thousand months. But by his munajaat and doa, even in those times of difficulty, Allah’s Dawat would remain, the Imam uz Zamaan would remain).
The munajaat of a Dai who offers remembrance and gratitude=For this greatest of favors and highest of gifts.=(The 52nd Dai Syedna Mohammed Burhanuddin RA bestowed the rutba of Da’wat to the 53rd Dai Syedna Khuzaima Qutbuddin TUS, and he offers shukur to Allah Ta’ala for this ne’mat.)
The munajaat of a servant who repents=Of his sins and transgressions.
Allah, I am a servant who has come to your door penitent=Asking forgiveness for his wrongs and misdeeds.
Laylat ul Qadr is Ma Fatema’s night=The one whose luminous miracles are magnificent
Laylat ul Qadr is the Imam’s Dawat=During his Seclusion; his Dais are Laylat ul Qadr’s holy hours.
In Laylat ul Qadr I come to you=With the loftiest of vasilas and sincerest of intentions
I take the vasila of Mohammad, Ali, Fatema=Hasan and Husain—Mawali with exalted virtues
Of the Imams in their progeny, chieftains among people=And of Tayyeb Imam, my Mawla, Mawla of all the worlds
Of the Duaat Mutlaqeen, each and every one=Their walayat my treasure for the hereafter
Of Syedna Taher Saifuddin, sun among them=The virtues of all past Dais are gathered in him
Of Syedna Mohammad Burhanuddin, his successor=Possessor of the most sublime of qualities
Allah, sanctify Mawla Burhanuddin’s soul=Ascended to the Garden of Eden
My dearest wish: to perform ziyarat of my two benefactors=Walking to Rauzat Tahera on my eyes
By their vasila, Allah, forgive us our sins=And grant us your godly blessings
Grant me qurratul ayn in my children=Give them tawfeeq always to earn virtues
Allah, protect Dawat ul Haqq=From the machinations of malevolent spirits
Nine dushmans creating havoc in its lands=Seize them, Allah, and punish them for their crimes.=(Allah Ta’ala says in the Qur’an: “Nine individuals are creating havoc in the land [of Da’wat]; they do not do any good” – Surat al-Naml: 48)
Grant us victory, Allah, Nasr e Azeez=And Fath e Mubeen, in all our suits and cases
Increase among the people the strength and izzat=Of noble mumineen and mumenaat
Make their trading and businesses profitable, establish their factories=Protect them from all kinds of calamities
In our sustenance and farming, grant us barakaat=Increase our pure and good means, our tayyebaat
Make our bodies healthy, Allah=Make our characters beautiful and strong
For any who have a spark of walayat remaining in them=Revive them, strengthen their belief
Give them tawfeeq to answer the Imam’s Dai=Without fear, with true hearts
Allah, make all mumineen partners in my doa=Children of this radiant Dawat, people of distinction
Allah, grant me a long life in the service of Imam Tayyeb=Fida for him, offering sincere khidmat.
Decree, Allah, Imam uz zamaan’s zuhoor=His deedaar is for Mumineen Eid
Bestow salawaat on Taha nabi and his pure progeny=Keeping them close to our hearts is the way to obtain najaat
