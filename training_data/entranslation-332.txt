I call to you in munajaat, my lord, offering you praise and gratitude=I pray to you, bowing down in your remembrance=(‘Munajaat’ means ‘speaking privately’ with Allah)
I take the vasila of Taha Nabi=Who brought Islam to the world
I take the vasila of his brother Maula Ali=His aide in revivifying God’s faith
I take the vasila of Maulatuna Fatema tuz Zahra=Best match for one with exalted virtue
Laylat ul Qadr, showering barakaat=Symbol for her, luminous radiance
I take the vasila of the two sanctified, purified imams, Hasan and Husain=Who displayed matchless qualities of haqq
I take the vasila of their progeny, progeny of the prophet Mohammed=Our imams, each Allah’s manifestation
I take the vasila of Tayyeb Imam=Though concealed, present amongst us through his Dais
I take the vasila of the Tayyebi Dais=Each Dai commands according to Tayyeb’s wishes
I take the vasila of the sun among the pure Dais=Whose title was Saifuddin, whose name was Taher
I take the vasila of my benefactor, my Maula Burhanuddin=I will always offer thanks to him
[With all these vasilas I pray to you:] Allah, in this jihad against the enemy=Make me victorious through your nasr-e aziz, grant me success with your fateh mubeen
Foil their plots and their malevolence=Seize them swiftly, lord, and vanquish them all
Imam uz zamaan is my Maula and master=By God, he is powerful over all things
He is the best protector of his Dawat=He is the best helper for his Dai in all battles
He gazes at his servant who loves him dearly=With eyes full of kind caring, bestowing inspiration (ta’eed)
Allah, forgive my sins=Conceal my faults
Allah, grant us abundant sustenance always=Make the flowerbeds of our hopes bloom
Allah, grant me a long life to serve my Imams’ Dawat=Make their Misr – the city of Da’wat - prosper
Grant me qurratul ayn in my children=Make them pure and good
Clothe them and all mumineen=In the rich garments of piety
Allah, grant tawfeeq to those who were once mumin=Dispel their darkness
Shower salawaat upon Taha Nabi and his noble progeny=As long as the rain pours down from your sky
