I pray to you, my Lord, for you are near.=I call to you, my Lord, for you will answer.=The Qur’an says: “If my servants ask you about me, tell them: I am near. I answer the prayer of those who pray to me.” (Baqarah 2:186)
My hope is in you. My trust is in you.=You are the best guardian. You are the best beloved.
I stand at your door, my Maula, your servant, a prisoner.=All manner of dangers surround me.
I stand at your door, my Maula, your heart-broken servant.=The Age has struck me with calamities.
I stand at your door, my Maula, a needy mendicant—=humble, lowly, rejected, and far from his homeland.
I stand at your door, my Maula, your grief-stricken servant—=emaciated, abased, weak, and desolate.
You have promised forgiveness for sins=to any who come to you in sincere repentance.
I have come to you, Allah, in repentance, not for a single transgression—=Indeed, my sins are as numerous as the sands.
Allah, my Allah, take my hand!=I am drowning in sin.
Allah, save me from this Sea of Hayula.=Show kindness. Let me return to Paradise!
O my Lord, forgive all my sins.=I am your servant come to you in atonement.
Allah, have mercy on me. Allah, grant me aid.=I am a man whose hair old age has greyed.
O Sustainer (raziq), erase my poverty and write in prosperity—=even if it is not in my providence (naseeb)
For you erase what you will and write in what you will.=Whomever you will, you reward.
Since you possess the Mother of the Book (Ummul Kitaab).=Your servant calls to you. He has no refuge but you.=The Qur’an says: “Allah erases what He wills and writes in what He wills. He possesses the Mother of the Book.” (Ra’d 13:39)
I seek intercession (vasila)—the Five Pure Ones.=Our hearts are made tranquil by their remembrance.=The Qur’an says: “Harken! Hearts are made tranquil by Allah’s remembrance.” (Ra’d 13:28)
—The Night of Qadr, Fatema.=Her virtues are magnificent.
—Her sons and the Tayyib of my Age.=Allegiance (walayat) to them is my soul’s triumph and fragrance.
Love for them is the good deed and the treasure.=Enmity for them is the evil deed and the sin.=Amirul Mumineen SA said “the good deed is love for us. The evil deed is enmity toward us”
—Them and their vicegerents (na’ib).=Each Dai serves in his place.
—Their heir, Syedna Qutbuddin.=Keen minds are amazed by his virtues.
Syedna Khuzaima Qutbuddin—like Syedna Taher Saifuddin and Syedna Mohammed Burhanuddin—was trustworthy and noble.
The chain (silsila) of Nass-appointment continues.=Impossible that the lights of Nass fade!
I am his son, Taher, heir to the legacy of the Dais through Nass.=I am born of that noble legacy.
I am the servant of the high lords, the Aale Mohammed.=My life is perfumed by their remembrance.
I serve them. I lay down my life for them—=For this, I am ready to spill my blood, to emaciate my flesh.
I say this, as Syedna Abdeali Saifuddin said:=“By my life, my condition is a wonder, what a wonder!”
By the inspiration (ta’eed) of the Master of the People, Tayyib of the Age,=My arm’s reach is never short and my judgment hits the mark.
My jihad is for Truth.=I never weary. Battles do not exhaust my strength.
I possess courage.=Slackness never touches me.
I call out to Maula Ali—=His name makes dangers light and difficulties small.
Allah, grant me your aid (nasr-e-azeez).=If you grant me aid, then surely victory (fath-e-mubeen) is near!
Allah, make me one of those who offer you thanks.=By your grace, my house is filled with blessings.
Allah, make me one of those who offer you praise.=By your providence, my farm is fresh and fruitful.
By your kindness, my people are supporters (ansaar) of Truth.=My radiant children are morally upright.
The wellspring of my knowledge is pure.=The drink of the enemy of Dawat is dirty.
He leads your servants astray by his deceptions—=People’s doubt and confusion keeps increasing.
Destroy him speedily!=After death, he will attain total loss and blazing flames.
Include the children of my Dawat in my supplication.=Allah, I ask you to forgive their sins.
Allah, pardon them and grant their wishes.=O Guardian, grant them your protection.
Grant tawfeeq to those who were part of our community.=Let them give misaaq to your Dai, Taher Fakhruddin.
Allah, shower blessings of salawaat and salaam on Taha Nabi’s descendants=As long as the rain wind blows.
