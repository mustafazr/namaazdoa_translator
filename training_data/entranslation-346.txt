I have seen the light of Kashf (The Age of Revealing) in the night of Satr (The Age of Concealment)=With the ta’eed (inspiration) of Mustafa’s grandson, Imam uz zaman Tayyib
Behold! In this way, the light of the master of our age=Illumines the dark night, like the sun in the blazing day
He is the world’s refuge, sanctuary from perdition, dispeller of calamity=Ocean of generosity, quencher of thirst, eradicator of harm
Imam uz zaman is the crescent moon of faith, pure embodiment of Eid=My prayer, my fasting, my breaking of the fast, are all bound to him
His virtues cannot be counted=His merits shine like the stars
He is Rasulullah Taha Mohammed’s grandson=In his radiant character, he resembles Mohammed
He is the banner, the guide, the Imam of our Age=He is the son of Ali and pure Fatema
The descendant of Ali—Hasan and Husain’s father—is exalted over all=He is the best of the world’s people, nomads and city dwellers
He is the embodiment of the Garden of Eden=From which rivers of true knowledge flow
He is the qibla (prayer direction) of truth in this world=He possesses true excellence and esteem
Is there another for whom Allah revealed verses of praise=That the faithful recite in the surahs of the Qur’an?
Allegiance to him is an obligation mandated by Allah=Love for him is the most valuable treasure
He is the reason for Allah’s creation=The people of intelligence are dazzled by his virtues
He appointed the Dais in his place=They are his deputies in the Age of Concealment
Each Dai l-mutlaq is a man of honor and discernment=He is a benefactor, a man of great learning and openness
Their heir, Maulana Qutbuddin=A goldmine of good and compassion
The light of truth shone from his visage=A full moon illuminating the night
A gushing wellspring of generosity, sagacity, and kindness=A towering mountain of truth, knowledge, and fortitude
He was a caring, loving father to all mumineen=His gifts more precious than pure gold
May the Lord of the Throne exalt his sanctity=His coming made the flowerbed of Paradise blossom
His remembrance gives life to right, truth, and intelligence=I remember him every day of my life
He appointed me with Nass to serve the Master of the Age=I give thanks to him every moment of my existence
Tayyeb Imam’s gift of honor to me in Eid ul Fitr=Is his ta’eed, which flows unceasing
I am Taher Fakhruddin, his vicegerent=His service is my highest honor (fakhr)
I am his servant, and it is my hope=That he will accept my poem of felicitation for Eid
In the name of pure Fatema’s son, O Lord=I sincerely supplicate you for aid and victory (nasre azeez, fathe mubeen)
I ask you to destroy the enemies of right guidance=Swiftly, O God of the Throne, with one fell swoop
Allah’s salawaat on Taha, his pure progeny and their Dais=As long as the rainclouds pour down.
