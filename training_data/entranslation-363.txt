O Progeny of the Prophet, grant me aid!=Seize all the insolent enemies who spread corruption.
O my Masters, grant me aid swiftly!=Do not tarry in doing so, do not delay.
Aid your servant!=Indeed he has hastened his journey toward you.
Grant aid, and send the soldiers of your aid,=For the troops of the enemy have surrounded us.
Grant us mercy, O Progeny of the Prophet,=Who was sent as mercy personified to all worlds.
Ya’ooq and Nasr, the two evil ones, and Yagooth,=Have led many astray, O Progeny of Mustafa and Murtaza.

O my Masters, unsheathe the Zulfiqaar,=The sharp, cutting sword. All others are blunt and soft.
By the Lord of creation, you are the deep secret behind creation,=Heaven (Tooba) for the one who seeks it.
If you were not the reason behind creation,=Not a single thing would have been created.
The Prophets and Messengers achieved excellence through you,=Through you did Adam and Sheeth gain glory.
Sons of revelation,=The Qur’an and the Prophets sayings praise you.
O Progeny of Taha, your nobility is ancient, deep-rooted,=The nobility of all others is recent.
O Allah’s chosen, your follower is good and pure.=Your enemy is the worst of mankind, wicked.
The one who mixes Nifaaq (hypocrisy) with his Imaan (belief) in you,=Dissolves his faith and invalidates it, just as water dissolves salt.
If one resolves to perform Hajj towards you,=He should abstain from obscenity, sinfulness, and argument.
You are rains of magnanimity, seas of generosity,=And during battle, lions of the forest.
You appointed the true Duat when you entered concealment,=By their watering, the greenery of true guidance is abundant.
If it were not for the Duat watering the pastures of true guidance,=They would dry out.
The Duat occupy the palace of Itlaaq (Full authority),=Just as you have entrusted it to them.
May the rainclouds of God’s salawaat (blessings) water your residence,=As long as rainfall waters the lands.
