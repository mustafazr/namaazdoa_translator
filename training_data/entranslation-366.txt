Allah’s Wali, you are the crescent moon.=Your luminous vision, O majestic one, is Eid.
Descendant of Nabi Taha, descendant of the best of women.=You fulfil supplications before being asked.
Descendant of Mustafa’s brother Haydar who killed disbelief,=“mighty in powerful wrath” (Surat Ra’d Ayat 13).
Imam of the Age,=heir of Mustafa’s pure descendants, best of all descendants.
Your lofty rank, O master of the world,=is great and unmatched.
All those who love you are lofty in Paradise.=All those who hate you burn in the Fire.
Great victory for the man who bears witness=that you are his Mawla in this world and after death.
Allah will shower him with blessings=that will never end.
O light of the Lord of the Throne, O blazing noon-day sun.=Your beauty and splendor are radiant.
Great loss for those who go looking in the sky=for the sighting of the moon, they are in error
What darkness in error,=when they shoot arrows at each other over the sighting!
O Tayyib of the Age, Imam of right guidance.=I offer my life and my wealth to you.
I am your servant, your Dai who calls to you.=I seek to make you happy in every circumstance.
You are Allah’s rope.=My rope—through the age—remains forever attached to you.
The chain of Nass-appointment has come to me,=by your high command, without break.
In all affairs, O my Imam,=I rely on you.
I beseech, O my master, a kind glance from you,=for it is a treasure for the Return
Send down to me your flowing blessings=from the sky of your pouring grace, O generous one.
O unifier of the congregation of Mumineen, grant me aid and victory,=grant me grace, splendor and perfection.
Accept my felicitations, O true Eid!=O light of the Lord of the Throne, light of the crescent moon.
May our Lord shower salawaat blessings on you,=as long as deep-rooted mountains remain on the earth.
