Abdul Qadir Hakimuddin Mawla=Mazoon of the Glorious Creator’s Dai
Hafizul Quran, you grasped perfectly, The Quran’s hidden and manifest meanings
In every branch of learning and knowledge=You attained the utmost expertise
Your worship brightened the Merciful Lord’s masjids=Your direction invigorated the radiant Dawat’s assemblies
Pride of all Mazoons who served=Tayyib Imam’s radiant Dawat
Refuge for the seeker of sanctuary=Best shelter, best support
Most eloquent poet and biographer=Who narrated the virtues of the pious Dais
You always remembered your Dai=And he always remembered you
Syedna Ismail Badruddin bestowed on you, The noble, exalted and precious rutba of Mazoon
You were the strongest pillar and vizier=For the Dais you served
Light for the believer’s intellect=In both knowledge and practice
Balm for the mumin’s heart, lump in the munafiq’s throat=Torment for the heart of the kafir
Hakimuddin Mawla, son of Bawa Mulla Khan Saheb, Who was a devoted servant of the Dai
Best of humans—your high character, Mirrored the seerat of the angelic inhabitants of the heavens
How many shining miracles you performed=O Wise Hakim of the believers
How many brilliant virtues you possessed=None can count them
How many excellent qualities you gathered, And praiseworthy, noble, magnificent traits
And beautiful, auspicious, blissful features, And consummate, lofty, exemplary attributes
How often you articulated principles of sincere devotion=For the Dais, with your tongue and your heart
How often you devised virtuous customs, Which enormously benefited the people of belief
How many Dais hail from your line=Pious, chaste and pure
What noble leaders, each one of them, What splendid vessels of piety and purity
What immaculate forms of pristine perfection, What magnificent models of acceptance and goodness
True scholars disseminating the knowledge of Mohammed’s progeny, Wise sages, from the line of the wise sage, “Hakim”
I am your descendant, and I beseech God Almighty=For forgiveness with your wasila
I ask this for all believers, may the Merciful Lord, Grant their wishes, with His infinite grace
May He answer the prayer of every person among them, The first of them to pray as well as the last
How many jewels from your poems, most exquisite, How many gems from your treasury of knowledge
Reached your descendant, Taher Saifuddin, Cutting sword, Saif, of the Fatemi Imam
The Imam’s humble servant and faithful follower=Who takes refuge in his Mawla, and gives thanks for his favors
Allah has illuminated me with his inspiring ilhaam, as has the Imam,=Making me the finest disseminator of their precious wisdom
O Naseem breeze, carry our humblest salaam greetings, To Mawla Abdul Qadir Hakimuddin
How many visitors, many who pledge vows, come to your domed shrine=Their prayers are all answered
Comfort finds the one who takes refuge in you, Bliss (tuba) covers the believer who comes to your shrine
I am a humble servant of the progeny of Mohammad and Ali, Both equal in the scales of luminous virtue
Descendants of Fatema Zahra=Possessors of virtues that shine like the stars
Pure and chaste, Tayyib and Tahir, in their origin, Clear manifestations of our Merciful Lord
Their priceless gems of knowledge=Are my most cherished riches	
Allegiance, walayat, to Mohammad’s progeny and their Dais, Is my treasure—most valuable investment of all
May Allah shower blessings, salawaat, on the prophet and his progeny, As long as raindrops pour down from the clouds
