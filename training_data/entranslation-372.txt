We offer Almighty Allah praise and gratitude=We worship Him—exalted be His name—and remember Him
We offer Him praise—He sent to us an emissary=Whose mercy covers the world
Mohammed is His messenger and prophet=Whose pulpit is elevated above all the prophets’ pulpits
Haidar is his wasi and aide=God’s namesake and manifestation
Fatema is his consort and hujjat=Her khutba crushed his enemy
Hasan and Husain are balm for her eyes, qurrat ul ain=Their grandfather revealed their virtues
The true Imams are from their progeny=Our love for them is a treasure we safeguard for our salvation
Tayyeb Imam’s robes are pure and clean=His essence, his core, is celestial 
In their concealment, their Dais are their gateways, baab=I transcribe these lines of praise on this Eid day
Sun among the Dais, Syedna Taher Saifuddin is my father=Day and night, always, always, I remember him
His successor Syedna Burhanuddin is my benefactor=I am grateful for the ne’mat he has bestowed
Our splendor, our glory, stems from their grace=Its abundance covers us in triumph
Aid your servant, O master of the world=He will be victorious if you grant him victory
Foil the enemy’s plot and humble him=He will be vanquished if you vanquish him
Grant me fateh mubeen=Raise the banner of truth
Triumph for the believer who recognizes and loves him=Ruin for the detester who denies him
Tayyeb Imam is my refuge in times of distress=When I drink, his Kawthar fills my cup
On Eid, I seek barakat from his repast, ma’idat=To break my fast
Lord of the World, destine his manifestation, zuhoor=His own Jame Azhar, Jame Anwar, long for him
By the command, amr, of Imam Amir’s son=I inhabit “the masjid whose foundation is piety” and make it prosper
I ask the lord of the throne to rain down salawaat=On the people of the cloak, Aal al ‘aba’
