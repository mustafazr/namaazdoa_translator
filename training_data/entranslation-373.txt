O Tayyib of this Age, Allah’s select=Rasulullah’s son, great of station
Son of Amirul Mumineen Murtaza (Ali)=Son of the pure Batul (Maulatuna Fatima), the best king
Heir to the legacy of noble ancestors=Equal to them in honor
Kaaba for worshippers, Baitullah (the House of Allah)=I perform Hajj to you with my soul
Veil for the world’s glorious Creator, Lord of Majesty=Your virtues are infinite
Angels bow their foreheads=In your exalted presence
Those who do not practice Tayyib’s religion=Are astray in the valley of error
You guide humans to the true path of reason=Those who stray from you are like cattle and sheep
You command all that is good=And forbid evil
Allah Ta’ala, the lord of the throne boasts to his angels=Of your radiant, noble Dais
Maulana Qutbuddin has arrived into your celestial presence=Seeing you in person, face to face
Grant him closeness and intimacy, Mawla=And sanctity and blessings
Send down to me, your servant, your celestial ma’idat=Speak to me directly
Grant me mighty aid and a clear victory, nasre azeez and fathe mubeen=Awaken those who are sleeping
Seize the oppressors in a swift punishment=In their folly, they try to demolish your house, the Baitullah
Protect God’s religion, the rightly guiding Dawat=Safeguard the blossoming flowerbed of Truth
We offer our lives for you and offer thanks=with our tongues and our hearts for your favors and blessings
We seek refuge in you, Mawla of the world=We string together pearls of your praise
You are Eid, O Imam=You unite our community, and are unique, peerless
Glance at us with kindness this Eid=Nourish us with the fruits and streams of paradise
May Allah, shower on you, O Nabi Mustafa’s descendant (Tayyib Imam)=His most bountiful and abundant salawaat blessings
