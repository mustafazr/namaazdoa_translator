The gifts of the Tayyib of our Age are blessed=His favors are a precious treasure
He is master of both the worlds, son of Ali Murtaza=The world has been created for him
Like his grandfather, Nabi Taha Muhammad=He is God’s chosen one (Mustafa), the best of the people, trustworthy
He is the son of Fatema, whose Prophetic house=Jibra’eel came to sweep
He is God’s lofty proof for His servants=He, none other, is the Qur’an’s companion
The Soorats of Ha-Mim and Ta-Sin were revealed in his honor=The Soorats of A’raf and Ya-Sin were revealed in his praise
The Creator would not have created His creation if not for him=He is its concealed secret
He is Allah’s vicegerent on His earth=Allegiance (walayat) to him is linked with allegiance to Allah
Almighty Allah has taken Tayyib as His veil=Allah’s mysteries and His mystery of mysteries are preserved in him
Such is his preeminence: If he wishes something, Allah wishes it too=Such is his excellence: If he says, “Be,” a thing comes to pass
Master of the people, refuge from perdition=An Ark in the sea of error for the people of faith
From the World of First Creation, always, and without a doubt,=Aid and empowerment come to him
His fayz flows to us at all times=Wellsprings of true knowledge gush
One who loves him with full heart, with sincerity, is rewarded with Paradise=His enemy is accursed
His prayer—quenching the thirst of one who petitions him—=Flows like sweet, clear water
O Light of the Lord of the Worlds and His face=Darkness is vanquished when eyes see you
Healing our sick, O Messiah of the Age,=Your right hand strokes the ailing
The world is a house of misfortune and calamity=Except that you provide security from its fears
May the Lord of the Worlds grant you a blessed Eid ul Fitr=We all celebrate today, and religion too
O son of Muhammad and his Wasi, rise up and conquer the lands!=Allah will give you aid!
I am your humble servant, O Master of the world=Your beneficence and kindness is the treasure I seek
I am the son of your Dai Syedna Qutbuddin=His truth was as unshakeable as the mountain
May Allah shower salawaat on the Prophet and his pure descendants=As long as pouring clouds water the earth
