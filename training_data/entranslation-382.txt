Maulana Saifuddin is the Imam’s unsheathed sword, saif,=His name AbdeAli, his station lofty and exalted
Sun among the Dais, Duaat Mutlaqeen, Who are all seated on the high throne of Dawat
Unparalleled in learning and skill,=Unrivalled in scholarship and expertise
Imam Tayyeb, son of Imam Aamir, made him commander, ameer, And what a strong commander he was!
Imam, Ameerul Mumineen, ameer of the believers, made him vizier, And what a wise vizier he was!
He was a Dai who called to Allah with His permission (raza),=Deputy of the Warner, of the Bringer of Glad Tidings (the Imam)
He was given a share in the Robe of Purity (tatheer ni chaadar), What a pure Dai he was!
He was informed and inspired by God, He knew what was concealed in people’s hearts
He was a guide unique in stature, He had no peer
High peaked mountain of loftiness,=Blossoming flowerbed of piety
Ocean of generosity, cave for refuge from perdition, lord of grandeur,=Forenoon sun, full moon lighting up the darkness
His knowledge was like the roaring sea, His strength was like Mount Yazbul or Mount Thabeer
His clay was composed of the clay of light left over, From the creation of the pure, rightly guiding Imams
In comparison with his nobility,=All patricians are plebeian and common
The lord of his age granted him wisdom, “Truly, wisdom is the most abundant goodness”
How well he organized the rightly guiding Dawat, With perception, expertise, and proficiency
He walked the path,=Of the blessed Fatemi Imams
His resolve was so firm,=That all difficulties melted away
He defended Dawat like a lion defending his den,=From the treacherous munafiq and the evil attacker
He managed all affairs with full control, He removed all obstacles and hardships
He granted all who solicited whatever they wished for, He fed “the poor, the orphan, and the captive”
He is the best intercessor for faith’s children, On the fearful day of judgement
He is the best helper for those,=Who are mauled by the fangs of fate
Help me, help me, O Maulana AbdeAli Saifuddin,=You are the believer’s refuge, sanctuary for the seeker of succor
How many times have you relieved the hardships, Of those whom fate has given its bitter cup
Let your follower be blessed with the tidings of paradise,=Let your enemy be informed of impending hellfire
Your servants are truly free—they will live forever in heaven,=“Their garments will be of silk”
“They will stretch out on couches”,=“No burning sun there, no scorching heat”
“Their faces will be happy” on the morrow, That for others will be a scowling, terrible day
You are AbdeAli, servant of Ali Murtaza, Who was graced with Taha Nabi’s Nass on the day of Ghadeer
Glad tidings come to the one who pledges allegiance to him:=At death, he will be received by the two bearers of glad tidings, Mubashshir and Basheer
Woe to the one who engages him with malice: At death, he will be received by the two terrible ones, Munkar and Nakeer
Allah is Maulana AbdeAli Saifuddin’s helper,=And His noble angels are his aides
What a sharp blade, saif, death bringer,=That slashes the enemy throngs
What a magnificent leader, sayyid,=With brilliance, authority, and a powerful voice
The true sultan, cool shade of the lord of the throne,=Who protects all God’s servants from the world’s blazing heat
The one singled out by the lord of the age,=One who has great virtue
He passed away, now resident in the high palaces of paradise,=With the true, noble Imams
May Allah bless him with His salaam, Perfumed with the fragrance of the celestial abode
May Allah’s barakaat always water his grave,=With continuous, pouring rain
He performed Nass on Maulana Mohammed Izzuddin, Lofty of stature, flowing sea of generosity
Seven Dais later I succeeded to,=Syedna AbdeAli Saifuddin’s honored station
A hundred years lie between us,=And they point toward a deep secret
Allah’s barakaat and bliss, sa’aadaat,=Have multiplied for me, by His grace
It is from Allah that I seek aid, nasre azeez,=It is from His Wali Imamuz zaman that I seek sustenance
My father gave me the title, laqab, Saifuddin, Harnessing the barakat of Syedna AbdeAli Saifuddin’s title
My father was [the 49th Dai] Syedna Mohammed Burhanuddin,=His generosity and goodness covered the world
Syedna AbdeAli Saifuddin is worthy of all the praise,=Directed to the pure Imam
His virtues are legion,=They cannot be encompassed by poetry or prose
He is the first—the absolute first in virtue, Even though he lived in the final age
I offer him abundant thanks for his favors, But I cannot recompense even a tenth of a tenth
I follow his traces, always,=And Allah is my Mawla and the best helper
I implore my Mawla for inspiration, ta’eed,=In small affairs and large
“I am a mendicant, faqeer, begging for,=My Mawla’s generous charity”
May Allah shower salawaat on Taha Nabi and his progeny,=As long as the full moon lights up the darkest night
