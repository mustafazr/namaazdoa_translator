Imam of the Age, one who stands in recognition of you as Arafah=Has truly recognized you (arafah)
Because of you, grandson of Taha Mustafa,=My soul gains closeness (muzdalifah) to the Lord of the House
To you, the Speaking, Living House of God,=My soul bows in continued worship
It offers gratitude for your favors=Acknowledging that it cannot do justice to them
It drinks continually from the sea of your gifts=O sea of light and guidance
Son of Mustafa and Murtaza=Whom he raised on his shoulder  (to strike down idols from the Ka’ba).
Son of Mawlana Imam Tayyeb=Heir of his pure nobility
What a pious, godly, pure son=Joy of the hearts of his forbears
He is the Baitullah=Populated by celestial angels
He is the Ka’batullah=Surrounded by God’s mercy
Hajar-e-Aswad would kiss his feet=If it knew he had come
He is the Mustajar and Multazam, refuge for all people=Seek him and you will inhabit the chambers of Paradise
Whosoever stands upon the Arafat of his knowledge=Has learned its sublime truth
To gain your wish (muna), seek the Mina of his blessings=You will be showered with his wondrous gifts
The soul that stands atop the Safa of his pure love=Obtains his sublime grace
The man who stands atop his Marwa=Wins safe haven
Whosoever describes him with attributes of the Lord of the Throne=Has described him well
God’s attributes, almighty and transcendent,=Apply to him. God himself is beyond imagination
Exalted his stature, whosoever assigns partners to him=Is errant and a fool
After his two grandfathers, he is the best=Of those in whose praise tongues and lips move
How many Holy Books has the Lord revealed=Extolling you, beloved son of Fatima Zahra
During your concealment, your Dais=Shine like stars in the night, dispersing its darkness
They split open the nacre of the Wise Quran=To reveal—by your inspiration—its iridescent pearls
I, your servant, offer gratitude for the honor of Hajj=That you, O Ka’batullah, have bestowed
May Allah grant you, O Master of the World,=The continual gift of his salaam
