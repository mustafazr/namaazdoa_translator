Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm=Lofty, abundantly excellent=In it Nabi Mohammed proclaimed Nass of succession=Upon his brother, son of his uncle=Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm
Today is the greatest of Allah’s Eids=Today is the day of legendary excellence=In it the luminous light shone forth=Our Mawla named it with this name=Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm
Today is the day of revelation=Today is the day of clarification=Disclosure of the high, radiant station=Of the Master of Arab and Persian=Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm
Today the lord of the Throne perfected his religion=Stretched out his right hand to take the Misaaq pledge=Victory to the man who fulfills his pledge to Allah!=He wins the finest prize=Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm
Today is the day of the pledge that was pledged=Today is the day of the assembly that was gathered =In it, waters of forgiveness are sipped=In it, rains of mercy pour down=Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm
In it, Taha Nabi ascended the minbar=Sovereign of all prophets=With him a Mawla who was truly his like=One named with Allah Ta’ala’s name=Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm
He stood up and delivered a sermon to the people:=“I, from among you, am your Mawla=For whomsoever I was Mawla, this Mawla is henceforth his Mawla=This is my lord’s command and my command”=Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm
Is there another who is Allah’s select?=Is there another who is Zahra’s match?=Is there another who is Mohammed’s brother?=For whom he said: “Your flesh is my flesh”?=Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm
What a wondrous embodiment of the ultimate=Allah’s true hand and eye=His watersprings of generosity and knowledge =Gushed forth in this world=Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm
He was when ‘be’ was not yet existent=His munificence and generosity covered all creation=It was right that they bow to him in prostration=His name is exalted beyond reach of imagination=Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm
Call out to Ali, call out to Ali=Almighty Allah’s saint, wali=Swiftly the Master of Creation will come to your aid=Dispelling all cares and misery=Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm
O Ali, named with the Merciful’s name=O Mawla, dispel my cares and misery=From the grip of hellfire’s flames, free my body and soul=My soul, father, mother—all fida for you=Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm
I am your servant, I seek refuge in you=In you and none other, I secure my hope=I have full conviction that I will gain salvation, najaat=That Allah will forgive all my sins=Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm
Allah, shower with barakaat all mumineen =In this day, and all people of conviction, yaqeen=Who have pledged their Misaaq to Allah with full yaqeen=Participating in this auspicious tradition of deen=Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm
Shower salawaat upon Ahmad and his brother=Each sought the same thing that was sought by the other =And upon his pure progeny, incandescent in color=As long as the horizon is lit by the star=Today is the Eid of Ghadeer-e-Khumm
