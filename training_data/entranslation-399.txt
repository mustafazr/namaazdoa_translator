My soul goes on Hajj to Husain in Karbala.=My heart melts with his remembrance.
I grieve for you, O Allah’s Wali.=You were slain thirsty in Karbala.
I weep for you, who were killed unjustly in Taff.=My tears flow like a waterfall.
I mourn for Husain’s beloved Abbas and Ali,=and for the suckling baby who shuddered upon his hands.
Fulfil our wishes, by your kind benevolence!=Dispel the hardships of all who are in turmoil!
O companion, this religion lives on because of Husain.=Remember Husain whenever you hear a muezzin chant the name of God.
O Naseem breeze, convey my humble greetings=in Karbala to the martyrs of Taff.
