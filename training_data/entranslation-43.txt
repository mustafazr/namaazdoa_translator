O possessor of exalted virtues, I take support in you=Tooba for the one who has you as his Maula
Tooba for the one who, scared and fearful,=Complains of his trials to the Majestic One
When the wind blows around him=He falls down prostrated
He has no illness or disease=Greater than his love for his Maula
When he comes to his lord alone in the darkness of the night=Allah favours him with his generosity and brings him close
When he complains of his sorrows and needs=Allah answers him, calls out to him:
Labbayk, servant of mine, you are in my protection=I know of everything you speak
My angels savor your voice=Enough for you that I, too, hear it
Your prayer penetrates my veils=I forgive your sins
Ask me without fear or trepidation=Do not be afraid: I am Allah
