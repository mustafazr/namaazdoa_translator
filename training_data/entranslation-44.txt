Allah—You are the one in whom I place faith for every need.=You are the one to whom I complain of deep pain—have mercy on my plea.
Hark! You are my hope, the one who will dispel my suffering.=Forgive my sins, all of them, and fulfill my entreaties.
My provisions are few. I do not think they will get me to where I need.=Do I weep for lack of provisions, then, or the far distance yet to traverse?
I have come to you with deeds that are ugly and vile.=No one on earth has transgressed like I have.
But will You—who are my ultimate desire—burn me in the Fire?=What will I do then with my hope, what will I do with my fear!
