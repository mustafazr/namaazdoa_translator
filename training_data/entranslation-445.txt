Dafil Aafaat namaaz 
After Magrib sunnat, pray 2 Rak’at Dafil Aafaat namaaz as per the following niyyat:
Usalli salaate Dafil Aafaate wal baliyaate wal makhafaate min sharril layle wan nahare rak’atayni lillahi ‘azza wajalla ada’an mustaqbilal ka’batil haraami allahu akbar
First Rak’at: Pray "Al Hamdo" and "Wal Asre" surat
Second Rak’at: Pray "Al Hamdo" and "Inna Anzalna" surat
