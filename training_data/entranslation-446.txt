Tasbeeh ul A’zam namaaz 
After Isha’ nafilat, pray 4 Rak’at Tasbeeh ul A’zam namaaz as per the following niyyat:
Usalli salaatil Tasbeeh ul A’zame arba’a raka’atin lillahi ‘azza wajalla ada’an mustaqbilal ka’batil haraami allahu akbar
Qira’at Charti surat, preferably Naas, Falaq, Ahad, and Izaa Jaa’a (not Tabbat Yadaa)
Tasbeeh: “Subhanallah wa-l-hamdu lillah wa-la ilaaha illa l-lah wa-l-lahu akbar”
First Salaam:
First Rak’at: Begin namaaz with Niyyat and Takbirat ul-ihraam. Then:
1. Stand and pray Alhamdu and other Surat. Then pray Tasbeeh 15 times.
2. Say “allahu akbar” and go into Ruku’ and do Ruku’ tasbeeh. Then pray Tasbeeh 10 times.
3. Say “sami’a llahu liman hamidah rabbana laka l-hamd” and stand up straight again. Pray Tasbeeh 10 times. (Men should have their hands hanging beside them, women should place hands on chests, as they do while standing.)
4. Say “allahu akbar” and go into Sajda and do Sajda tasbeeh. Then pray Tasbeeh 10 times.
5. Say “allahu akbar” and sit and pray “allahumma-ghfir li …” Then pray Tasbeeh 10 times.
6. Say “allahu akbar” and go into second Sajda and do Sajda tasbeeh. Then pray Tasbeeh 10 times.
7. Say “allahu akbar” and sit with each leg folded beneath itself (ghayr mutamakkin, not like you sit in tashahhud). Pray Tasbeeh 10 times.
Second Rak’at
1. Say “allahu akbar” and stand up and pray Alhamdu and other Surat. Then pray Tasbeeh 15 times. 
2-6. Repeat as previous Rak'at
7. Say “allahu akbar” and sit as you sit in tashahhud and pray Tasbeeh 10 times. Then pray tashahhud and turn your head to the right and the left for salaam “al-salaamu laykum wa rahmat …”
Second Salaam: Begin namaaz with Takbirat ul-ihraam. Then repeat what you did in the First Salaa
