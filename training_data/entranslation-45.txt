I whisper to you in supplication, O you who are present in every place, Perhaps you are listening to my prayer,
My crime is enormous and my shame little, 
Maula, O my Maula! Which terrifying aspects should I recall, and which should I forget!
If there were nothing more to expect than death, that would suffice entirely, How much more so, when what comes after death is more formidable and more catastrophic! 
 Maula, O my Maula! Until when and up to when do I say, time after time, that I have taken your reproach to heart, and you find neither truth from me nor compliance, 
Help me, Allah, help me! Help me fight desires that overwhelm me, 
enemies who rabidly attack me, a world that has adorned itself in my eyes, 
and my own human soul—«the human soul incites to evil, save those to whom my lord shows compassion
Maula, O my Maula! If you have ever shown compassion to one like me, then show compassion to me, If you have ever accepted one like me, then accept me, 
O you who accepted Pharaoh’s magicians—accept me,
O you from whom I have only ever known good, 
O you who nourish me with blessings morning and night,
show compassion to me on the day I come to you alone, my gaze fixed on you, my deeds shackled round my neck, 
every person in the world withdrawing from me, Yes, even my father and my mother, and all for whom I strove and toiled, 
Maula, O my Maula! If you do not show me compassion, then who will? 
Who will comfort my desolation in the grave?
Who will prompt my tongue to speak when I am alone with my deeds? 
And when you ask me about those things you know better than I do, what then?
If I answer “Yes,” then where is the escape from your justice? And if I say, “I did not do those things!” you will respond: “Was I not witness over you?”
Maula, O my Maula! Forgive me, forgive me! Before the time comes to be clothed in black pitch!
And again, forgive, forgive! Before the time comes when hands will be shackled to necks! 
O best of forgivers, O most compassionate one
