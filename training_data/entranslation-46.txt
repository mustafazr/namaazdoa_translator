Then pray this:
Allah, Shower salawaat, barakaat, and salaam on Muhammad and Muhammad’s descendants—
And show compassion to my humbled state as I stand before you, Show compassion to my weeping in supplication, 
My alienation is from people, and my familiar comfort is in you,
O generous one, O you who existed before everything else, 
O generous one, O you who will exist after everything else,
Allah, do not expose my shame, for you know everything about me,  Do not punish me, for you are all-powerful,
Allah, I seek refuge in you from being dishonored in life, becoming a wretched spectacle at the time of death, 
finding an evil place of return in the grave, and being possessed of remorse on Judgment Day, 
Allah, I ask you for a good life, an auspicious death, and an honorable return without disgrace or shame,
Allah, your forgiveness is more expansive than my sins, Your compassion is more reliable than my deeds,
So forgive me and show me compassion, O living one who never dies, 
Truly, truly, there is no god but Allah, I prostrate before you, my lord, in worship and servitude,
O lofty one, O great one, Forgive the great sin, No one can forgive the great sin except you, O lofty one, O great one,
Allah, I seek refuge in you from failure, from carrying the burden of crime, 
Allah, turn away from me every sedition, evil, trial, deprivation, and destruction, Do this by your compassion, O most compassionate one, 
O you who are the lord of taqwa, the lord of forgiveness, O kind one, O compassionate one, 
You are kinder to me than my father, my mother, and all the people in the world,
Send me back today with wishes fulfilled, prayers answered, voice pitied, 
And all manner of calamities blocked,
And I stand before you seeking forgiveness, 
I ask you to free me from captivity and ease my affairs, O lofty one, O great one, 
Do this by your compassion, O most compassionate one, 
We place our trust in Allah for he is the best helper, He is the best Maula and the best support, 
There is no power or strength save in Allah, the lofty one, the great one, 
Allah, shower salawaat on our leader Muhammad and our leader Muhammad’s descendants,
As you showered salawaat on Ibrahim and Ibrahim’s descendants, You are the most praised, the most noble.
