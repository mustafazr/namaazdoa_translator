O Hearer of calls,=O Benefactor of wishes,
O Giver of requests,=O Solver of hardships,
O Sufficer in sorrows,=O Sender of blessings,
O Creator of the world,=O Creator of hearts,
For you O Lord of the universe,=Our bowings in our prayers and our prostrations,
O Lord forgive me my sins,=And efface my bad deeds,




















Accept from me,=My fasts and my prayers,
Grant me my wishes,=And bring to me my requests,
Cool my eyes,=In my sons and daughters,
Lord make me obedient to you,=As long as I live,
Praying namaaz,=Giving alms







I supplicate you through Taha Rasulullah,=And his progeny the Imams, for my salvation,
They are Allah's 'words',=What precious words they are,

They are my refuge and protection,=In life and in death,






Answer my prayers this night,=O Hearer of calls,
And answer the prayers of sincere mu'mineen (believers),=Men and women,
May Taha Rasulullah and his progeny,=Be blessed with the most complete of salawaat.
