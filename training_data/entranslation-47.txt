Mawla, my Mawla, you are my Mawla and I am your servant. Who will have mercy on a servant except his Mawla?
Mawla, my Mawla, you are mighty and I am humble. Who will have mercy on the humble except the mighty?
Mawla, my Mawla, you are the creator and I am your creature. Who will have mercy on a creature except his creator?
Mawla, my Mawla, you are a giver and I am a mendicant. Who will have mercy on a mendicant except a giver?
Mawla, my Mawla, you give succor and I request succor. Who will have mercy on one who requests succor except one who gives succor?
Mawla, my Mawla, you will remain and I will perish. Who will have mercy on one who will perish except one who will remain?
Mawla, my Mawla, you are eternal and I will cease to be. Who will have mercy on one who will cease to be except one who is eternal?
Mawla, my Mawla, you are living and I am dead. Who will have mercy on the dead except the living?
Mawla, my Mawla, you are strong and I am weak. Who will have mercy on the weak except the strong?
Mawla, my Mawla, you are great and I am small. Who will have mercy on the small except the great?
Mawla, my Mawla, you are my master and I am your slave. Who will have mercy on a slave except his master?
Mawla, my Mawla, you are my lord and I am your minion. Who will have mercy on a minion except his lord?
