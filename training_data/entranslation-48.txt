nisfullayl, 2 rakaats
Pray in the first rakaat alhamd and wash shams
Pray in the second rakaat alhamd and hal ataka
The niyyat:
I pray the prayer of the nisful layl, for Allah Taala, facing the Holy Kaaba - God is great.
If the time of this namaaz is not known or has passed, then take this niyyat:
I pray the prayer of the layl, for Allah Taala, facing the Holy Kaaba - God is great.
