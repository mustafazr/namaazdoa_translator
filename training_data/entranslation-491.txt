The progeny of Rasulullah are springs of blessings=They are the deliverance of the world in crisis,
They are the veils of Allah, in them Allah appears,=And they show signs (of Allah) and miracles,
They are the people of knowledge, and there is no doubt that with them,=Is the knowledge of the past, and of what is to come,
They are the beneficent ones, that trace their lineage back to Shaybata Hamd (Moulana Abdul Muttalib),=And to the Hashim of goodness, who laid forth food (for guests),
They inherited from their grandfather Mustafa (Rasulullah) and Murtaza (Ali),=The great virtues and graciousness of deeds,
It is the thresholds of the Imams that the angels wish to kiss,=What glorious thresholds they are
It is none other than them that Adam Nabi took the wasilah of,=At the time which he supplicated near God with their names,
Through them accept O Allah,=From the people of faith, their prayers and their fasting,
May Taha, the one whom Allah is pleased with, and his shining progeny,=Be blessed from the Lord of worshippers with prayers (salawaat),
