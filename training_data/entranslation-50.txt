Allah, I ask you for pardon, mercy, triumph, forgiveness, blessings, and salvation. 
Allah, I ask you in the name of the First Being you created from the light of your oneness, and the Being after him who emanated from him
both of whom you made the reason why all things came into being and the cause for their existence. Help me reach my spiritual home and my luminous station.
Care for me with your mercy. Aid me with your support. 
Open my inner eye to recognition of you. Help me reach my goal in worship of you. Do this by your mercy, O most compassionate one.
Pray after every salaam:
Pray a voluntary prayer in the deep of the night— your lord will likely raise you to a glorious station.
