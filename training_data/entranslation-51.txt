Allah, I ask you for pardon, mercy, triumph, forgiveness, blessings, and salvation. 
Allah, I ask you in the name of your angels whom you have made residents of your kingdom, inhabitants of your skies, and planners of your universe. 
Have mercy on me and forgive me. Help me reach my spiritual home and my luminous station. Grant me the lofty companionship of your angels.
Guide me to walk the path toward my luminous station and my spiritual home. Do this by your mercy, O most compassionate one. 
Pray a voluntary prayer in the deep of the night—your lord will likely raise you to a glorious station.
