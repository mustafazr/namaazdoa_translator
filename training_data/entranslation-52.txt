Allah, I ask you for pardon, mercy, triumph, forgiveness, blessings, and salvation. 
Allah, I ask you in the name of Imam Amir bi-Ahkam Allah, Abu Ali al-Mansur, Amirul Mumineen, 
and his son Imam Tayyib Abu l-Qasim Amirul Mumineen.
May your salawaat be showered on his pure forebears, and his noble descendants, awaited all the way through to the Day of Judgment.
I ask you to forgive me. Help me reach my spiritual home and my luminous station.
Repeal my exile. Do not lengthen my journey, my lord. 
Make me one of those people whose deeds are beautiful, whose actions are pure, and whose return to the hereafter is blissful. 
Grant me the blessing of my Imam’s kindness and compassion. 
Help me to gain closeness to him and offer service in his presence
Place me among those who stand up in his support and protect his lands.  Do this by your mercy, O most compassionate one
Pray a voluntary prayer in the deep of the night—your lord will likely raise you to a glorious station.
