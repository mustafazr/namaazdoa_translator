Allah, I ask you for pardon, mercy, triumph, forgiveness, blessings, and salvation. 
Allah, I ask you in the name of the Master of the Day of Judgment, flowing sea of light.
possessor of auspicious character, destroyer of the cursed tree, 
the Qai’m of the age, master of indications and proof, 
Messiah of the community, and dispeller of hardship.
Allah, I ask you to have my recognition of him and belief in him be a source of guidance for me in this world and a treasure stored up for the next. 
Do not debase me on the Day of Judgment. 
Help me draw near to the prophets, the true, the martyrs, and the pious—what beautiful companions they would be!
That is bounty from Allah, and Allah is the all-knowing. Do this by your mercy, O most compassionate one.
