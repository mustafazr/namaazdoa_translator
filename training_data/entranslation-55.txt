Lord, I complain to you of my pitiful condition,=sincere in my supplication.
Bestow me a glance=that will be enough for me when facing dark nights.
A glance that will change the heat of high noon=to cool shade.
But I see a wall=between my plea and an answer.
If you turn away the hand=I have stretched out to you, O Glorious One,
Do I have another lord—O my hope!—=to whom I can turn my camels?
Will you give a bad answer,=to match my bad deeds?
Your mercy is wide enough to encompass forgiveness for my sins,=even if they are as heavy as the mountains.
Far be it for your clemency to give way=under my weighty transgressions!
My lord, I shall stand praying to you=as long as my form remains.
My intercessors are the pure Mawali=the Fatemi Imams.
Descendants of Nabi Mustafa,=refuges of salvation and protectors from error.
A group to whose rope of allegiance=my ropes have always been fastened
Until you untie the knots of my grief=by your bounty, unraveling all shackles.
