Then recite this dua:
O Lord, you reveal people’s deeds of charity and conceal their sins. 
O Lord, you do not seize offenders or tear veils. 
You are generous in bestowing forgiveness. You are magnanimous in your gifts. You are liberal in granting pardon.
You stretch out your hands in kindness. 
You listen to all whispered prayers. You receive all petitions. 
You grant favors before they are deserved. 
Our lord, our mawla, our sovereign, the final goal of our aspirations, 
I ask you this—do not deface our bodies with fire.
