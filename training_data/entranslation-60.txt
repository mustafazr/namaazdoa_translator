O you who answers the prayer of the afflicted man calling to you in the darkness=O you who removes harms and calamities and sickness
Pilgrims around your Baytullah fall asleep and awake=But your generosity’s eye, O living one, O sustainer, never sleeps
I call out to you, sorrowful, bewildered, anxious=By the sanctity of the Bayt and the Haram, have mercy on my tears
You are the Forgiver, so be generous with me, O my hope=Favor me with your generosity and munificence
If a sinner may not hope for your forgiveness=Then who will be generous with bounties to the disobedient?
Grant me, by your generosity, the charity of forgiving my sins=O you whom all people in the Haram supplicate,
