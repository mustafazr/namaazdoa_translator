Allah, You are my refuge in every calamity, and my hope in every catastrophe. You, in every disaster that befalls me, are my refuge and preparation.
In this great sorrow, my heart is enfeebled, my strategies have run out, friends have stepped back, and foes gloat.
It is because of my faith in You that I have been targeted, and my complaint is to You alone. My petition is to You, none else.
Deliver me and fend off the blow. You are the giver of every blessing, provider of every good thing, the ultimate one petitioned.
