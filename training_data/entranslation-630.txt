Allah, bestower of favors (no one bestows favors on You!), full of majesty and glory, resplendent with magnanimity and benefaction
There is no God but You. O Support for seekers of sanctuary! O Haven for seekers of refuge! O protector of those who scream for help! O He answerer of the prayer of the helpless!
O helper of the oppressed! O Succour for those who seek aid! O most merciful of the merciful!
Allah, if you have written me down in the Mother of the Book that is with you as a wretch and destitute, erase from me the label of destitution and wretchedness.
And confirm/establish me instead as blissful and wealthy.
Allah, if you have written me down in the Mother of the Book that is with you as deprived, one whose sustenance is scanty, erase my deprivation and the scantiness of my sustenance.
For you have said in your Noble Book, that has been revealed to your Prophet Muhammad, Allah’s blessings on him and his progeny—
“Allah erases what he wills, and confirms what he wills, for the Mother of the Book is with him” (Quran, Raʿd: 39)
You have also said—and you are the most truthful of speakers— ‘My mercy encompasses all things’ (modified from Quran, Aʿraf:156)
So then encompass me with your mercy, O most merciful of the merciful!
