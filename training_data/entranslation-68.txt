Praise to God who gleams in hearts with his majesty, conceals himself from eyes with his might, and is dominant over all things with his power. 
Eyes are not capable of seeing him and minds are not capable of reaching the depths of his majesty.
He is almighty in his majesty and grandeur. He is benevolent in his might, kindness, and eminence. 
He is sanctified in his beauty and splendor. He is exalted in his pride and glory.
He is joyful in his exaltation and his gifts. He is pure in his light and radiance. 
He is the creator for whom there is no peer. He is the singular for whom there is no equal. 
He is the one for whom there is no opposite. He is the eternal for whom there is no match. 
He is the god for whom there is no second. He is the maker for whom there is no partner. He is the sustainer for whom there is no helper. 
He is the first without end, the abiding without annihilation, 
the upright without exhaustion, the protector without termination, 
the originator without limit, the artisan without another, 
the lord without a partner, the maker without encumbrance, and the active without incapacity.
He is not limited in place nor bounded by time. 
He did not cease, nor does he cease, nor will he cease to be, in this manner, forever. 
He is the god who is living, sustaining, abiding, infinite, powerful, and wise.
After this dua, recite three times the following:
Allah, your humble servant stands before you, your mendicant stands before you, your supplicant stands before you.
Then recite as follows:
Allah, it is you whom the apprehensive fear. It is you whom the sincere beseech. They do this in awe of your majesty and in hope for your forgiveness. 
O creator of the universe, have mercy on those who cry out to you, forgive the transgressions of sinners, and be charitable to the repentant on the day of return, O generous one
