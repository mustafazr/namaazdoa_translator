Allah, I ask you for pardon, mercy, triumph, forgiveness, blessings, and salvation. 
Allah, I ask you in the name of your representative among your creatures, the one to whom you have entrusted your servants’ wellbeing, the most dear to you of all who possess human form and the closest among them to you,
the first human being you created, the first Adam, 
whom you made the repository of your words and the bearer of your worship, whom you housed in the most auspicious and noble of regions.
And in the name of the spiritual persons who succeeded him, who stood in his place and whom you charged with disclosing the true realities:
Help me reach my spiritual home and my luminous station. Do this by your mercy, O most compassionate one.
Pray a voluntary prayer in the deep of the night—your lord will likely raise you to a glorious station.
