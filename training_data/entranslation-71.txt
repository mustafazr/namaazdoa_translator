Allah, I ask you for pardon, mercy, triumph, forgiveness, blessings,  and salvation. 
Allah, I ask you in the name of your perfect ‘words’ who give direction toward you and guide toward you in the time of the true realities’ concealment,
your representatives on your earth and intermediaries between you and your creatures, 
Adam, Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, Isa, and their Wasis, Imams, and Hujjats.
Allah, I ask you in the name of Muhammad al-Mustafa, Ali al-Murtaza, Fatema al-Zahra, and Khadija al-Kubra.
Allah, I ask you in the name of the two Imams Maulana Hasan al-Mujtaba and Maulana Husain, the martyr of Karbala,
and the pure Imams in the line of Maulana Husain ibn Ali 
to Maulana Imam Tayyib Abu l-Qasim Amirul Mumineen
Allah, these are my intercessors to you. They are the ones whom you have made the repositories of your wisdom and proofs over your creatures. 
I ask you to have them take my hand in every calamity. 
Have my allegiance for them be a source of guidance for me in this world and a treasure stored up for the next. Do this by your mercy, O most compassionate one.
Pray a voluntary prayer in the deep of the night—your lord will likely raise you to a glorious station.
