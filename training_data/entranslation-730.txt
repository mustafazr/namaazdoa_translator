Allah, O provider of aid and succor. O dispeller of calamities.
O listener of prayers. O expeller of hardships and adversities.
Protect me, my family, and all the people of this house from droughts and epidemics,
Plagues and evictions, burning and calamities. Turn them all away from me.
Do this, by the honor of the house of your Nabi Muhammad Mustafa. Shower your salawaat and salaam on him and his descendants.
Allah, protect me, my family, my household, and the supporters of truth—protect them from every direction.
I supplicate you, my lord. Show compassion to your servants throughout the world. Relieve their difficulties in this pandemic.
