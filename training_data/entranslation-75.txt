Pray shafa and watar prayer, 
In the first rakaat pray alhamd and sabbi hisma rabbika
In the second rakaat pray alhamd and qul yaa ayyuhal kaafirun
If you do not remember sabbi hisma, then pray the surat of innaa anzalnaahu
Niyyat:
I pray the prayer of shafa, for Allah Taala, facing the Holy Kaaba - God is great.
