Eid, this holy month, all the days, and all the ages are made luminous by you,=even the radiant stars!
O sun of the religion of right guidance, O light of the Lord of the worlds=The Sacred House shines with your light, and so does the Hajar-e-Aswad.
So do fasting and breaking the fast, religion and its pillars, Haqq,=Shariat, the Quran, and its Surahs.
O you whose light dispels the darkness of Satr,=just as the moon brightens the dark night.
O Tayyib of the Age, O refuge of your devotees=your servant has no fear, for you are his support.
I bear witness that you are the master of all creation.=All creation bears witness to this, as do stones and earth.
Your remembrance—O best of God’s creation,=O Maula—is inscribed in the Surahs of the Wise Remembrance (Qur’an Majeed).
If virtues are a house, then you are its resident=distinction itself boasts of your distinction!
Your praise is my worship, O master=it is true speech adorned with rubies and coral.
Your fragrance wafts sweetly, your love is a stored treasure,=your gifts pour forth, your distinction is celebrated.
The Lord of the Throne has granted you wisdom—amazing wisdom,=not given to mere mortals!
You cultivate the crop of piety in the soil of your=radiant Dawat—its trees bear abundant fruit.
Send down to us, O Maula, the legions of heaven!=You are the one made victorious by Allah’s victorious aid.
You, O Gatherer of the Dispersed, are our Paradise.=You, O Maula, are the gushing stream of celestial knowledge.
You are the path of salvation and truth for the world.=Whoever comes to you is victorious.
O descendant of the Prophet,=O descendant of Ali—all minds are stunned when contemplating your great station.
Are you human? No, indeed, you are an angel!=Your qualities soar beyond the reach of mere thought.
Those who love you are truly human.=Those who do not bear allegiance to you are “sheep and cows.”
Your servant, Taher, is absorbed in your praise.=Your praise is my treasure, all else is but idle talk.
Felicitations, O Eid! I congratulate you for the presence of al Tayyib,=whose light, like the sun’s is brilliant!
O Allah, conquer for him all the lands of the earth.=Bestow upon him Salawaat blessings as long as raindrops fall.
