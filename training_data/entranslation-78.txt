Allah! O you who grants all petitions, fulfills all hopes, removes all hardships!
O you who dispels all sorrows! O compassionate one! O generous one! O mighty and glorious one!
O you who granted us the best of his treasures, faith, even before we asked,
do not refuse us the least of your treasures, forgiveness, when we beg for it now!
Allah! I ask you to bless Mohammed and his descendants, Aale-Mohammed.
I ask you to forgive our sins and grant us mercy.
I ask you, in your compassion, protect us from your wrath and pay off our debt.
I ask you to pardon us. You always pardon, you are always kind.
I ask you to write our names this very day among those whom you have freed from the Fire.
I ask you to give not our enemies and enviers a reason to gloat over our misfortune.
I ask all this through your compassion, O most compassionate one!
Allah, we have fasted for your sake. We have faith in you. We break our fast upon your bounty.
Accept our fast!
Our thirst has vanished, our hunger is sated,
while our reward remains, God willing, insha’allah!
