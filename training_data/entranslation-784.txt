Indeed, for me during (my) whole life in every adversity, with difficulty there has been ease, with difficulty there has been ease.=(In this bayt, Syedna Taher Saifuddin repeats the last sentence emulating the 5th and 6th aya mubaraka of Surat Alam Nashrah: “Inna ma’al usre yusran, inna ma’al usre yusra.”)
