Allah, this is the Dai of the pure Aale Mohammad.
Imams to whom you have bestowed the Book and wisdom, bequeathed a great kingdom.
And strung the pearls of their Imamate in the pearl-string of the Nass.
He is succor for refuge-seekers. A sheltering cave for believers.
Syedna wa Mawlana Abu Khuzaima Taher Fakhruddin.
Lengthen his lifespan Allah. Raise high his state.
Bestow on him your support and authority. Grant him an easy victory.
Watch over him with your eyes. Safeguard him with your protection.
Make us obedient to him. And help us strive to attain his happiness.
We ask this in the name of Syedna Mohammad and his pure progeny. Your salawaat upon them all.
Al Fateha.
