I pray to you, my Lord, you are the one who gives aid —=O you who are powerful over all things.
You are the all-knowing, you are the all-wise,=you are the most merciful, you are the most aware.
You are the most forbearing, you are the most generous,=you are the all-hearing, you are the all-seeing.
You are my rescuer, you are my refuge,=in you I place my trust, towards you is my journey.
You are my custodian and the best guardian.=You are my shelter and the best protector.
I am your humble servant, and you are my Creator.=I am a seeker of aid, and you are my helper.
I come to you repentant and remorseful —=pardon me, Allah, for my sin is enormous.
I come to you — forgive my sins and conceal my faults,=for my errors are many.
I knock on your pure door with hands of hope.=My Lord, I am a beggar before You.
Allah, open the door with your grace and bounty.=Grant me what I ask, and even what is in my heart.
Bestow upon me a gift from your generosity,=for I am a humbled and needy servant.
Be generous to me, Lord,=and grant me your favors and abundant bounties.
Grant me provisions,=let the rainclouds of your blessings pour down on me.
Lighten my burden of debts, discharge them for me,=you are my refuge and support.
Grant me blessing upon blessing,=I am your servant and Da’i, Taher.
Calm my heart, injured by fate’s ordeals,=and fed with bitter-tasting fruits.
Through your grace, replace hardship with ease,=for you that is effortless.
Have mercy on me, and bless me, Allah,=for this servant is weak and needy.
Protect me from grief=and calamities and distressful trials.
Guard me from the plots of=chaos-mongers and evil-spreaders.
Grant me aid, and grant me victory,=cast into the fire the enemy of faith.
Destroy the enemies of your religion,=you are their slayer and annihilator.
Protect me from my enemy’s scheming,=he is wicked and evil.
Grant delight to my eyes through my noble children,=my family, my household.
By your grace, place me among those,=who seek illumination from the sun of guidance (the Imam).
Grant me good health, Allah,=protect my body from the burning heat.
Include the children of my Da’wat in my prayer,=have them choose the righteous path.
And guide them to fulfil the pledge=that Nabi Mustafa took at Ghadeer.
By your grace, I have visited Rasul Husain.=I seek refuge always at his shrine.
How can I ever thank you?=When even a tenth of a tenth of the dues of gratitude cannot be fulfilled!
Allah, place me among those who are visited at death=by the Giver-of-Good-News, Basheer.
Do not place me among those who are visited at death=by the Bringers-of-Bad-News, Munkar and Nakeer.
Release my neck from the chains of physicality.=I am an exile, a captive, in this material world.
Allah, place me among those=who are clothed in Paradise with silk.
Reunite me (in Paradise), Allah,=with the righteous whose path I follow.
By the sanctity of the best of prophets,=and his brother and vizier.
Muhammad and Ali,=sun and moon lighting the heavens!
By Fatema, Laylat-ul-Qadr,=exalted in rank and honor.
By their pure descendants,=each Maula, Amir-ul-Mumineen.
By their heir, Tayyib of the Age,=whose glory is loftier than the moon.
He is embodied Truth, and I say,=in truth, that he has no equal in this world.
By their vicegerents,=each Da’i a sword drawn in the hand of Ali’s son.
By their heir, Syedna Qutbuddin,=whose radiant face shone like the moon.
I am your humble servant, Allah, and servant of Aale Muhammad,=seeker of their light.
I seek victory through Imam-uz-Zaman,=and I seek enlightenment through his inspiration.
Allah, shower salawaat and salaam on the descendants of Taha Nabi,=the one who warns (of punishment) and gives glad tidings (of Paradise).
