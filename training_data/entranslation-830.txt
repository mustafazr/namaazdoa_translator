O Tayyib, greatest in rank!=O Imam-uz-Zaman—Imam, son of an Imam.
You are my Kaaba—my Hajj is toward you,=O Wali of Allah, best of all the world.
I come to you to attain my hopes and wishes, O son of Taha Nabi,=O son of Fatema
O Son of Ali, Wasi of Taha Nabi,=whose words are the king of all words.
Heir of that Sun and Moon (Mohammed and Ali), their blessed descendant—=O sun of the guiding faith and its radiant full moon
O refuge of those who seek refuge, O noblest son of your forefathers,=our great and noble masters.
Your pleasure and acceptance are my deepest wish—=if I gain even a glimmer of your smile, I have triumphed.
You are Allah’s glorious Name—exalted be His name!=Uttering it cures even the most chronic afflictions.
There is no Imam in the world but you—=for by God you are the foremost—amam— in every perfection.
Your praise is inscribed in the Qur’an, your glory is a sky=above the sky, your virtue is exalted beyond measure.
O light of the Lord of the Throne—glorious be His lights!=In your face Allah may be seen, should you lift the veil.
The sun shines with the light from your radiant face,=and the clouds draw upon your bounty to give rain.
You are the one the Qur’an swears by—=You are the one symbolized by Allah’s Bayt al-Haraam (Baitullah).
Love for you is mandated—farz—from Allah, as the Quran has explicitly stated, without any ambiguity.=(Allah Ta’ala says in the Qur’an: O Mohammed, say: I do not ask from you anything in return-meaning I do not ask for anything in return for my guiding you towards the True Path--except that you love my family - Ali, Fatema, and their progeny)
You are the one by whose love=Allah accepts a servant’s prayer and fasting.
Whoever loves you with faith and sincerity shall gain=Paradise (Tuba) and a blessed return from this world.
O you who guard religion’s fortress=from the accursed and vile enemies
You who heal the sick and dispel blindness,=Messiah of the Age who gives life to the dead.
The ship of Dawat-ul-Haqq sails with your flowing aid,=cutting through every wave of the roaring sea.
Through your healing, O healer of the soul,=you cure the world from the sickness of physicality.
If my mind attempts to fly towards the heavens of your greatness,=it cannot reach even the lowest sphere—which is far beyond the Na’aam star.
You are Eid personified, O crescent of guidance—=the eye of my heart sees you in this lofty station.
Woe to the one who is blind to the light of your guidance!=Loss for the one who becomes a stubborn enemy!
My refuge, my sanctuary, my shelter—=O protector of your servant in this tumultuous age.
O helper of Mumineen, O commander—(Hakim Imam)=cut off the heads of the enemies with your sharp sword!
Unsheathe your sword - Zulfaqar,=and conquer the lands of the world, like Egypt and Shaam.
Strengthen the minaret of taqwa, and reinforce its foundation.=Enact strong vengeance against the enemies of faith.
How I wish I knew when you will emerge from concealment,=O Maula, and scatter this darkness!
I am your servant and Dai, Taher —O Master,=take my hand when death approaches.
My Maula’s inspiration, ta’eed, is the breath of life for me—=how do I give thanks when your favors keep pouring down?
Your humble servant’s reins are in your hands—It is not I who strikes the enemy, it is you who strikes!=(Allah Ta’ala addresses Rasulullah SAW in the Qur’an and says: “وَمَا رَمَيْتَ إِذْ رَمَيْتَ وَلَٰكِنَّ اللّٰهَ رَمَى” — O Mohammed, you did not strike; rather, it was Allah who struck.)
I am the son of your Dai, Syedna Qutbuddin.=I continue to remember his shaan, always and always.
My life is sacrifice, fida, for you. I long to see you,=O Imam-ul-Huda, even in a dream.
May the Saba breeze carry my Salaam to you, O Imam-uz-Zamaan=with sajadaat and humility.
May Allah forever shower His salawaat upon you. May the Salaam of Allah (al-Salaam) encompass you always.=(“al-Salaam” is one of the names of Allah, mentioned in this ayat in the Qur’an: هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِى لَآ إِلَـٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلْمَلِكُ ٱلْقُدُّوسُ ٱلسَّلَـٰمُ ٱلْمُؤْمِنُ ٱلْمُهَيْمِنُ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْجَبَّارُ ٱلْمُتَكَبِّرُ ۚ سُبْحَـٰنَ ٱللَّهِ عَمَّا يُشْرِكُونَ)
