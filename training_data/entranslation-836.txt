Are my eyes dry from this great calamity=Or have they gone blind from grief
The pain in my heart does not go away=It stings me like scorpion poison
The best of the world has gone from this earth=Mohammed, who held the highest station
Allah’s Dai, Allah’s proof (Burhan)=Whose virtues shone like the stars
His loss has changed the look of my face=My relatives don’t recognize me
My day has become darkest night=For he has gone, like the sun setting in the West
O Allah, sanctify Syedna Burhanuddin’s soul=And grant him all his wishes in paradise
Today is the auspicious Milad day of our Imam=O lord of all gifts, grant us barakat on this day
And today is the day the faithful give misaaq=To Imam uz zamaan and his Dai, his vicegerent
Syedna Qutbuddin, Khuzaima=Vicegerent of the prophet’s pure sons
They are my refuge in difficulty and my succor=When the storm gathers in from every side
And their vicegerents, who shelter the world, are my harbor=My haven, my treasure, in calamity
For Syedna Qutbuddin is their heir, son of Aqa Taher=Mansoos and best companion of Syedna Burhanuddin
His visage gleams like lighting=Wakening my slumbering aspirations
For every need, in every situation=I turn my caravans in his direction
I am his humble servant, fida on him=My soul, my wife, my family, all my loved ones
O God, grant him every comfort and the best of health=And grant him life long as the shining stars
As for his enemies—cut them up and destroy them=And give them over at the end to perdition and humiliation
May Allah shower salawaat on Taha and his noble progeny=Numerous as drops in rainclouds
