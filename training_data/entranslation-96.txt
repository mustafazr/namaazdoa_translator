All praise is for Allah Who showered us with blessings so that we may praise Him and thank Him
Praise that is ever-lasting and continuous, that grows and rises
Its beginning is not cut
And its last does not end
Nothing destroys it. And nothing of it is cut off
Praise that is bountiful, that it is worthy of Him
Oh Allah! Veil me from vices
And turn away from me all difficulties
And ease my hardships
Oh Most Merciful One
Oh Glorious and Generous One
