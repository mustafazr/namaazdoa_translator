Allah! There is no God but you, Lord of the seven skies.
Lord of the mighty throne.
The First and Last for all things.
Creator of all. Provider for all.
Originator of the skies and earth.
Maker of angels, messengers with wings.
You are powerful above all.
Allah! Shower salawaat on Mohammed, Your servant and messenger, leader of the faith.
leader of the good.
prophet of mercy.
Allah! I ask you to protect me from poverty and destitution, from difficult situations.
from the catastrophes brought by day and night.
And if it has befallen already but not yet struck me, then deter it from me. 
Save me from the Fire.
